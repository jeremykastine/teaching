Class Policies

This master document is for future quarters. For the requirements that apply to your current class, see the syllabus and the resources listed under your course on the Teaching page.

Attendance and absences

I record attendance in Quickly. To request an excused absence, tell me which class meeting you will miss or have missed, and ask to be excused. Contact me in person or by email in advance when possible, or as soon as possible after the absence.

I generally mark the absence Excused based on your request. I typically ask for a reason only if I am concerned about a pattern of absences. Your attendance record may have consequences under Life University’s attendance policies.

When you miss class, take these steps to stay caught up:

  1. Check your section’s up-to-date class schedule for the meeting you missed. Identify the topics, resources, and any deadlines listed for that date.
  2. Review the assigned materials and the Essentials review. Rework class examples on your own and use the relevant practice test problems to check your understanding.
  3. Check Blackboard for announcements and due dates. Ask me about any material or instructions you still have questions about.

Participation extra credit

For each class meeting in which you fully participate, I add 1 percentage point to your course average. Full participation means arriving on time, staying for the full meeting, and actively taking part in the assigned classwork. Arriving late, leaving early, or participating only part of the time may reduce the bonus for that meeting.

For example, if your course average is 82% and you earn full participation credit for five meetings, your average rises to 87%. I calculate your participation extra credit periodically and enter it manually in Blackboard so you can see how much you have earned.

An Excused mark records your attendance status only. If you want participation credit for a missed meeting, you must complete the makeup oral quiz at the first class meeting you attend after the absence. Come to that meeting ready to take the quiz.

The quiz takes about two to three minutes. Prepare the short list of facts, procedures, and examples identified in the Essentials review for the missed meeting. I will choose one item at random and ask you to reproduce it from memory. You may need to explain a step aloud or show a few steps on paper.

If you successfully complete the quiz, you earn the 1-point bonus for the missed meeting, and I record the result as Excused + Participation Credit in Quickly. I will record the result on a short form, give you a copy, and keep a copy for my records. If you need more practice, I will tell you what to review.

Weekly homework check-in

Complete one check-in in Blackboard each week. Report what you did outside class over the previous week, estimate your total study time outside class, and attach a clear photo of one page of your own work from that week. Homework credit is based on completing the check-in.

Choose study activities that address the material you need to practice. The check-in will ask you to indicate which activities you completed, such as:

  • Attempted practice test problems independently and checked your answers.
  • Corrected mistakes or tried a fresh version of a practice test.
  • Reworked class problems or worked through the Essentials review.
  • Used ChatGPT or another study tool to generate similar problems, then solved them yourself.
  • Reviewed course materials, practiced with a classmate, or sought help on a topic.

Use the practice tests as your main guide for test preparation. Work the relevant problems on your own, check your answers, and revisit any problem types you still find difficult.
