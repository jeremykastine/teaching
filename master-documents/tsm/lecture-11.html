Lecture 11: Linear equations without fractions

Assessment coverage TSM 098 test 3

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to reveal or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

Simplify each side first. Then use operations on both sides to collect variable terms and isolate the unknown.

Key definitions

A solution makes the original equation true. Equivalent equations have the same solutions. An identity holds for all real inputs for which the expressions are defined.

Compare the solutions of \(3x=0\), \(0x=3\), and \(0x=0\).

Dividing the first equation by \(3\) gives \(x=0\). For the second, \(0x\) is always \(0\), so it never equals \(3\). For the third, \(0x\) is always \(0\), so every real number works.

Solve and check \(-7x=42\).

Divide both sides by \(-7\). The solution is \(x=-6\). In the original, \((-7)(-6)=42\).

Solve \(4x-7=2x+5\).

Subtract \(2x\) and add \(7\) on both sides to get \(2x=12\). Divide by \(2\) to get \(x=6\).

Solve \(5x-3+2x+8-x=2x+7-x+3x+4\).

The left side simplifies to \(6x+5\) and the right side to \(4x+11\). Subtract \(4x\) and \(5\) to get \(2x=6\). The solution is \(3\).

Solve \(3(2x-1)-2(x+4)=-(x-4)\).

Distribute all three factors. The left becomes \(4x-11\) and the right becomes \(-x+4\). Add \(x\) and \(11\) to both sides to obtain \(5x=15\), so \(x=3\).

Solve \(2(3x-1)+5=6x+3\).

Distribution gives \(6x-2+5=6x+3\). Subtracting \(6x\) produces \(3=3\). Every real \(x\) is a solution.

Solve \(4(x-2)-x=3x-7\).

The equation becomes \(3x-8=3x-7\). Subtract \(3x\) from both sides. The resulting statement \(-8=-7\) is false, so there is no solution.
