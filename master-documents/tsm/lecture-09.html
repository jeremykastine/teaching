Lecture 9: Multiplying and dividing fractions and rational expressions

Assessment coverage TSM 098 test 2 · Numerical fractions only · TSM 099 test 2

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to reveal or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

Rewrite division by a nonzero number as multiplication by its reciprocal. After that rewrite, regroup factors to make cancellation easy.

Key definitions

The reciprocal of a nonzero fraction is the fraction with numerator and denominator exchanged. An original divisor must be defined and nonzero.

Numerical fractions and chains

Evaluate \(3\cdot4\div5\cdot6\div(2\div5)\) by rewriting division as multiplication.

Division by \(5\) contributes \(1/5\). Division by \(2/5\) contributes \(5/2\). Combine those factors first, then halve \(4\).

\[3\cdot4\cdot\frac15\cdot6\cdot\frac52=3\cdot2\cdot6=36\]

Evaluate \(8\div3\div4\div(2\div15)\) by grouping the divisors, then show the same calculation with fractions.

The consecutive divisions by \(3\) and \(4\) amount to division by \(12\). Dividing by \(2/15\) multiplies by \(15/2\). Cancel before multiplying.

\[8\div3\div4\div(2\div15)=8\cdot\frac1{12}\cdot\frac{15}{2}=\frac{8\cdot15}{12\cdot2}=5\]

Simplify \(\frac{7\cdot10}{3\cdot5}\div\frac73\).

Multiply by \(3/7\), then cancel \(7\) and \(3\). The remaining quotient \(10/5\) is \(2\).

Multiply \(-\frac{14}{15}\cdot\frac{25}{21}\).

Reduce \(14/21\) to \(2/3\) and \(25/15\) to \(5/3\). The product is \(-10/9\).

Divide \(\frac16\div\left(-\frac49\right)\).

Keep the numerator \(1\) and multiply by \(-9/4\). Reduce \(9/6\) to \(3/2\).

\[\frac16\div\left(-\frac49\right)=\frac16\left(-\frac94\right)=-\frac38\]

Rational expressions

Multiply \(\frac{x^2-4}{x^2+5x+6}\cdot\frac{x+3}{x-2}\) and state restrictions.

The original denominators exclude \(-3,-2,2\). After factoring, all three factors cancel.

\[\frac{(x-2)(x+2)}{(x+2)(x+3)}\cdot\frac{x+3}{x-2}=1,\qquad x\ne-3,-2,2\]

Divide \(\frac{x^2-9}{x^2-x-6}\div\frac{x+3}{x+1}\) and state restrictions.

The denominators exclude \(3,-2,-1\). The divisor is zero at \(-3\), which is also excluded. Multiply by the reciprocal, then cancel \(x-3\) and \(x+3\).

\[\frac{(x-3)(x+3)}{(x-3)(x+2)}\cdot\frac{x+1}{x+3}=\frac{x+1}{x+2},\qquad x\ne-3,-2,-1,3\]

Simplify \(\frac{2x^2-8x}{x^2-16}\cdot\frac{x+4}{4-x}\).

Factor \(2x(x-4)\) and \((x-4)(x+4)\). Cancel \(x-4\) and \(x+4\) and keep the original exclusions. The result is \(2x/(4-x)\) for \(x\ne\pm4\).
