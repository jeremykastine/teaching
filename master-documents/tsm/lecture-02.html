Lecture 2: Combining like terms and like factors

Assessment coverage TSM 098 test 1

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to reveal or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

First decide whether the pieces are being added or multiplied. The operation determines what can be combined.

Key definitions

Terms are the pieces joined by addition or subtraction. Factors are pieces multiplied together. The coefficient is the numerical factor. For a positive integer exponent, the exponent counts copies of the base as a factor.

Like terms

Terms with the same variable part are like terms. Combine their coefficients and keep the common variable part.

Simplify \(3x+5x-2x\).

Each term is a multiple of \(x\). The signed coefficients add to \(3+5-2=6\).

\[3x+5x-2x=6x\]

Simplify \(4x^2-3x+7-6x^2+x-2\).

The square terms combine to \(-2x^2\), the linear terms to \(-2x\), and the constants to \(5\).

\[4x^2-3x+7-6x^2+x-2=-2x^2-2x+5\]

Like factors and evaluation

Multiply numerical factors. For powers of the same base multiplied together, count all the repeated factors. Parentheses determine what belongs to the base.

Simplify \(2x^2\cdot3x^3\) and explain why the result is different from \(2x^2+3x^3\).

The product has coefficient \(6\) and five factors of \(x\), so it is \(6x^5\). In the sum, \(x^2\) and \(x^3\) are different variable parts, so the terms cannot combine.

Simplify \((-3x)(-2x^2)(4x)\).

The coefficient is \((-3)(-2)(4)=24\). Count \(1+2+1=4\) factors of \(x\). The answer is \(24x^4\).

Compare \(-3^4\) and \((-3)^4\).

In \(-3^4\), the base is \(3\). Take the opposite of \(81\) to get \(-81\). In \((-3)^4\), the base is \(-3\). Four negative factors have product \(81\).

Evaluate \(2(x-3)^2-x\) at \(x=-2\).

Use parentheses when substituting. Evaluate inside the power, square, multiply, then subtract.

\[2((-2)-3)^2-(-2)=2(-5)^2+2=50+2=52\]

Evaluate \(18\div3\cdot2-4+1\).

Division and multiplication have the same priority and go left to right. Then subtraction and addition go left to right.

\[18\div3\cdot2-4+1=6\cdot2-4+1=9\]
