Lecture 15: Linear equations with numerical fractions

Assessment coverage TSM 098 test 3 · TSM 099 test 3

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to reveal or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

Combine each side into one simplified expression before moving a denominator. Keep the entire numerator together.

Key definitions

An LCD makes fractions on the same side combinable. Moving a whole-side denominator means multiplying both sides by that nonzero number and simplifying.

Solve \(\frac{x}{3}+\frac{x}{6}=5\).

Combine the left side as \(3x/6=x/2\). Multiply the whole equation by \(2\) to get \(x=10\).

Solve \(\frac{x+5}{4}=x-1\).

The denominator \(4\) divides the entire left side. Move it to multiply the entire right side.

\[x+5=4(x-1)\]

Distribution gives \(x+5=4x-4\), so \(9=3x\) and \(x=3\).

Solve \(\frac{3x-1}{4}=\frac{x+2}{3}\).

The remaining denominators divide whole sides, so multiply through by \(12\). Keep both numerators grouped.

\[3(3x-1)=4(x+2)\]

Then \(9x-3=4x+8\), so \(x=11/5\).

Solve \(\frac{x-2}{3}-\frac{x+1}{6}=\frac{x-1}{4}\).

The left becomes \([2(x-2)-(x+1)]/6=(x-5)/6\). Simplify the common multiplier of the two denominators to get \(2(x-5)=3(x-1)\). Thus \(2x-10=3x-3\), giving \(x=-7\).

Solve \(\frac{x+2}{3}+1=\frac{2x-1}{6}\).

The left is \((x+5)/3\). Multiplying by \(6\) gives \(2(x+5)=2x-1\). Canceling \(2x\) leaves \(10=-1\). There is no solution.

Solve and check \(2-\frac{x-1}{5}=\frac{2x+1}{3}\).

The left becomes \((11-x)/5\). Then \(3(11-x)=5(2x+1)\) gives \(33-3x=10x+5\), so \(x=28/13\). Both original sides equal \(23/13\).
