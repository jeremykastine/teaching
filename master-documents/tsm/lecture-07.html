Lecture 7: Nonmonic trinomials by guess and check

Assessment coverage TSM 099 test 1

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to reveal or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

Factor a nonmonic trinomial by making a short list of binomial candidates. The first and last products fix the choices. The cross products decide which choice works.

Key definitions

Nonmonic means the leading coefficient differs from \(1\). In \((px+r)(qx+s)\), the leading coefficient is \(pq\), the constant is \(rs\), and the middle coefficient is \(ps+qr\).

Factor \(6x^2+11x+3\) by testing candidates.

Use positive constants. The following rows cover the allocations up to switching the two binomials.

\[\begin{array}{c|r}(p,q;r,s)&ps+qr\\(1,6;1,3)&9\\(1,6;3,1)&19\\(2,3;1,3)&9\\(2,3;3,1)&11\end{array}\]

\[6x^2+11x+3=(2x+3)(3x+1)\]

Factor \(6x^2-7x-3\).

Try the factor pairs \(2,3\) for the leading term and \(-3,1\) for the constants. The cross products \(2x\) and \(-9x\) give \(-7x\).

\[6x^2-7x-3=(2x-3)(3x+1)\]

Factor \(8x^2-14x+3\).

Both constants must be negative. With leading factors \(4\) and \(2\), use constants \(-1\) and \(-3\), giving cross products \(-12x\) and \(-2x\).

\[8x^2-14x+3=(4x-1)(2x-3)\]

Factor \(6x^2+13x+6\).

Leading factors \(2,3\) and constants \(3,2\) give cross products \(4x\) and \(9x\).

\[6x^2+13x+6=(2x+3)(3x+2)\]

Factor \(-12x^2+22x-6\) completely.

Pull out \(-2\) first. In the remaining trinomial, \(2x-3\) and \(3x-1\) give cross products \(-2x\) and \(-9x\).

\[-12x^2+22x-6=-2(6x^2-11x+3)=-2(2x-3)(3x-1)\]

Determine whether \(2x^2+x+3\) factors over the integers.

The leading factors are \(2,1\). The positive constants must be \(1,3\) in either order. The cross sums are \(7\) and \(5\), so neither is \(1\).
