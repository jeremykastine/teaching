Lecture 6: Monic trinomials and differences of squares

Assessment coverage TSM 098 test 1 · Monic trinomials only · TSM 099 test 1

Overview

Use signed factor pairs to reverse binomial multiplication. Perfect squares provide a separate pattern for two-term differences.

Key definitions

A monic quadratic has leading coefficient \(1\). For \((x+p)(x+q)\), the middle coefficient is \(p+q\) and the constant is \(pq\). A difference of squares \(u^2-v^2\) factors as \((u-v)(u+v)\).

Monic trinomials

Factor \(x^2+9x+20\).

The positive factor pairs of \(20\) are \((1,20),(2,10),(4,5)\). The last pair sums to \(9\).

\[x^2+9x+20=(x+4)(x+5)\]

Factor \(x^2-2x-24\).

The factors have opposite signs. The pair with magnitudes \(4\) and \(6\) gives \(-2\) when \(6\) is negative.

\[x^2-2x-24=(x+4)(x-6)\]

Factor \(x^2-10x+25\).

The factors have the same sign, both negative. The pair \(-5,-5\) has product \(25\) and sum \(-10\).

\[x^2-10x+25=(x-5)^2\]

Decide whether \(x^2+x+6\) factors over the integers.

The factors of \(6\) would both have to be positive to give a positive sum. The pairs \((1,6)\) and \((2,3)\) give sums \(7\) and \(5\). Neither gives \(1\), so there are no integer binomial factors.

Differences of squares

Factor \(9x^2-49\) and verify the result.

The squares are \((3x)^2\) and \(7^2\). The cross products cancel when multiplied.

\[9x^2-49=(3x-7)(3x+7)\]

Factor \(2x^2-50\) completely.

First take out \(2\), then factor the difference inside.

\[2x^2-50=2(x^2-25)=2(x-5)(x+5)\]

Compare \(x^2-16\) and \(x^2+16\). Factor each over the integers if possible.

The difference gives \((x-4)(x+4)\). For the sum, a positive constant requires same-sign factors and a zero linear coefficient requires opposite nonzero factors. Those conditions cannot both hold.
