Lecture 17: Graphing linear equations

Assessment coverage TSM 099 test 4

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to reveal or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

A line consists of all the ordered pairs that satisfy its equation. Use points, intercepts, or slope to draw it.

Key definitions

An ordered pair gives the horizontal coordinate first. An intercept is a point where the graph meets an axis. Slope is vertical change divided by horizontal change.

Determine which of \((2,-1),(-1,2),(0,3)\) solve \(2x+y=3\).

The left sides are \(4-1=3\), \(-2+2=0\), and \(0+3=3\). Thus \((2,-1)\) and \((0,3)\) are on the line.

Graph \(y=2x-1\) using a table for \(x=-1,0,1\).

Substitution gives \((-1,-3),(0,-1),(1,1)\). Plot them and draw the straight line through them.

Graph \(3x+2y=6\) using intercepts.

Set \(y=0\) to get \(x=2\). Set \(x=0\) to get \(y=3\). The line joins \((2,0)\) and \((0,3)\) and continues in both directions.

Graph \(y=-\frac23x+1\) from the slope and intercept.

Start at \((0,1)\). Move right \(3\) and down \(2\) to \((3,-1)\), or left \(3\) and up \(2\) to \((-3,3)\).

Compare the graphs and slopes of \(x=2\) and \(y=-3\).

The first graph contains points with \(x=2\) and every real \(y\). It is vertical with undefined slope. The second contains points with \(y=-3\) and every real \(x\). It is horizontal with slope zero.

Sketch \(y=-x\) and label both intercepts.

The line passes through \((-1,1)\), \((0,0)\), and \((1,-1)\). Both intercepts are the same point, \((0,0)\).
