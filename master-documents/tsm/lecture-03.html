Lecture 3: The distributive property

Assessment coverage TSM 098 test 1

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to reveal or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

Use distribution to change a product involving a sum into a sum of products. Then look for like terms.

Key definitions

In the distributive property, the outside factor multiplies every term inside the parentheses. A minus sign directly before a group acts as a factor of \(-1\).

Expand \(-4(2x-3)\).

The outside factor multiplies both terms. The second product has two negative factors.

\[-4(2x-3)=-8x+12\]

Remove the parentheses and simplify \(7x-(3x-5)\).

Distribute \(-1\) to both \(3x\) and \(-5\). Then combine the \(x\) terms.

\[7x-(3x-5)=7x-3x+5=4x+5\]

Expand \(2x(3x-4)\).

The first product has two factors of \(x\). The second product has one.

\[2x(3x-4)=6x^2-8x\]

Simplify \(3(2x-5)-2(x+4)\).

Multiply the first group by \(3\) and the second group by \(-2\).

\[3(2x-5)-2(x+4)=6x-15-2x-8=4x-23\]

Simplify \(-(2x^2-x+4)+3(x^2+2x-1)\).

Distribute both factors to all three terms in their groups. Group the resulting terms by degree.

\[-(2x^2-x+4)+3(x^2+2x-1)=-2x^2+x-4+3x^2+6x-3=x^2+7x-7\]

Expand and simplify \(-2x(3x^2-x+4)+x(5x^2-2x)\).

The two distributed products give \(-6x^3+2x^2-8x\) and \(5x^3-2x^2\). The square terms cancel.

\[-2x(3x^2-x+4)+x(5x^2-2x)=-x^3-8x\]
