Lecture 1: Signed-number operations and multiplication facts

Assessment coverage Foundational practice for both courses

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to reveal or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

Use multiplication facts in both directions. Seeing a number as a product helps with division today and with factoring later.

Key definitions

A factor is a number in a product. The opposite of a number is the same distance from zero on the other side. Subtracting a number means adding its opposite.

Signed sums and differences

Evaluate \(-8+13-7\).

Move 13 units right from \(-8\) to reach \(5\). Subtracting \(7\) then moves 7 units left.

\[-8+13-7=5-7=-2\]

Evaluate \(-6-(-9)\).

Subtracting \(-9\) has the same effect as adding \(9\).

\[-6-(-9)=-6+9=3\]

Evaluate \(-12+5+12-9\) by pairing opposites first.

The \(-12\) and \(12\) sum to zero. The remaining \(5-9\) is \(-4\).

Multiplication facts in both directions

Two negative factors give a positive product. One negative and one positive factor give a negative product. For a nonzero divisor, read a quotient as a missing factor.

Evaluate \((-7)(8)\) and use the same multiplication fact to evaluate \((-56)\div(-7)\).

The magnitudes give \(7\cdot8=56\). One negative factor gives \((-7)(8)=-56\). Since \((-7)(8)=-56\), the missing factor in the quotient is \(8\).

List the positive factor pairs of \(56\), and give two negative integers whose product is \(56\).

The pairs are \((1,56),(2,28),(4,14),(7,8)\). The next possible first factor after \(7\) is \(8\), which repeats the last pair in reverse. The numbers \(-7\) and \(-8\) also multiply to \(56\).

Evaluate \((-63)\div9-(-4)(6)\).

The quotient is \(-7\) because \(9(-7)=-63\). The product is \(-24\). Subtracting \(-24\) adds \(24\).

\[(-63)\div9-(-4)(6)=-7-(-24)=17\]

Fill the blanks and explain how the same fact helps with both: \(8(\square)=-72\) and \(72\div(-8)=\square\).

Both blanks are \(-9\). The fact \(8\cdot9=72\) gives \(8(-9)=-72\) and \((-8)(-9)=72\).
