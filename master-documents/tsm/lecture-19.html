Lecture 19: Solving linear systems by substitution

Assessment coverage TSM 098 test 4

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to reveal or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

Replace one variable with an equal expression from the other equation. Once you find one coordinate, substitute back for the other.

Key definitions

Substitution replaces a variable with a value or expression equal to it. A dependent system represents the same line twice. An inconsistent system has no shared solution.

Solve \(y=2x-1\) and \(x+y=8\) by substitution.

Replace \(y\) in the second equation with \(2x-1\). Then \(x+(2x-1)=8\) gives \(x=3\) and \(y=5\). Check \(3+5=8\) and \(2(3)-1=5\).

Solve \(2x-y=5\) and \(x+3y=13\) by substitution.

Isolate \(y=2x-5\). Then \(x+3(2x-5)=13\), so \(7x=28\) and \(x=4\). Back-substitution gives \(y=3\). Both original left sides are \(5\) and \(13\), respectively.

Solve \(x=7-2y\) and \(3x+y=11\).

Substitute \(7-2y\) for the complete \(x\).

\[3(7-2y)+y=11\]

Then \(21-5y=11\), giving \(y=2\) and \(x=3\). The answer is \((3,2)\).

Solve \(y=3x+2\) and \(6x-2y=1\) by substitution. Explain what the graphs do.

Substituting gives \(6x-2(3x+2)=1\), or \(-4=1\). The lines are distinct and parallel. There is no solution.

Solve \(y=4-x\) and \(2x+2y=8\) by substitution. Describe the full solution set.

Substitution gives \(2x+2(4-x)=8\), which is true for every \(x\). The lines coincide. Every pair \((x,4-x)\) is a solution.

Solve \(2x+y=4\) and \(x-2y=3\) by substitution.

Isolate \(y=4-2x\). Then \(x-2(4-2x)=3\) gives \(5x=11\) and \(x=11/5\). The other coordinate is \(-2/5\). The original left sides become \(22/5-2/5=4\) and \(11/5+4/5=3\).
