Lecture 4: Multiplying polynomials

Assessment coverage TSM 098 test 1 · TSM 099 test 1 · Binomial products only

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to reveal or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

Multiplication of polynomials extends distribution. Each term of one factor pairs with every term of the other.

Key definitions

A binomial has two terms. A trinomial has three. In a product of monomials, multiply coefficients and add the exponents of like bases.

Binomial products

Expand \((x+4)(x+7)\).

The four products are \(x^2,7x,4x,28\). Combine the middle two terms.

\[(x+4)(x+7)=x^2+7x+4x+28=x^2+11x+28\]

Expand \((2x-3)(3x+5)\).

The cross products are \(10x\) and \(-9x\). The constant product is negative.

\[(2x-3)(3x+5)=6x^2+10x-9x-15=6x^2+x-15\]

Expand \((-2x+3)^2\).

Write two identical factors. Each cross product is \(-6x\).

\[(-2x+3)^2=4x^2-6x-6x+9=4x^2-12x+9\]

Expand \((5x-2)(5x+2)\) and describe what happens to the linear terms.

The cross products \(10x\) and \(-10x\) cancel.

\[(5x-2)(5x+2)=25x^2-4\]

Products with trinomials

These larger products are assessed in TSM 098. Write one row of products for each term of the shorter factor.

Expand \((x-2)(x^2+3x-4)\).

The \(x\) row is \(x^3+3x^2-4x\). The \(-2\) row is \(-2x^2-6x+8\).

\[(x-2)(x^2+3x-4)=x^3+x^2-10x+8\]

Expand \((x^2+x-1)(x^2-2x+3)\).

The rows are \(x^4-2x^3+3x^2\), \(x^3-2x^2+3x\), and \(-x^2+2x-3\). Add the coefficients of each power.

\[(x^2+x-1)(x^2-2x+3)=x^4-x^3+5x-3\]
