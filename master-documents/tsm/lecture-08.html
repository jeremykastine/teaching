Lecture 8: Simplifying fractions and rational expressions

Assessment coverage TSM 098 test 2 · Numerical fractions only · TSM 099 test 2

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to reveal or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

A fraction stays the same when you divide the numerator and denominator by the same nonzero factor. In a rational expression, record where the original denominator is zero before you simplify.

Key definitions

A rational expression is a quotient of polynomials. A restriction excludes an input that makes an original denominator zero. Opposite factors differ by a factor of \(-1\).

Numerical fractions

Reduce \(\frac{-42}{60}\).

Both \(42\) and \(60\) are divisible by \(6\). The reduced magnitudes \(7\) and \(10\) share no factor greater than \(1\).

\[\frac{-42}{60}=-\frac{7}{10}\]

Reduce \(\frac{84}{126}\) in stages.

Divide both by \(7\) first, then by \(6\).

\[\frac{84}{126}=\frac{12}{18}=\frac23\]

Evaluate \(\frac07\) and explain why \(\frac70\) is undefined.

The first quotient is \(0\), since \(7\cdot0=0\). For the second quotient, there is no number that produces \(7\) when multiplied by \(0\).

Rational expressions

Simplify \(\frac{x^2-9}{x^2+x-6}\) and state restrictions.

The denominator factors as \((x+3)(x-2)\), so \(x\ne-3,2\). Cancel the shared nonzero factor \(x+3\).

\[\frac{x^2-9}{x^2+x-6}=\frac{(x-3)(x+3)}{(x+3)(x-2)}=\frac{x-3}{x-2},\qquad x\ne-3,2\]

Simplify \(\frac{(x+4)(x-2)}{(2-x)(x+1)}\) and state restrictions.

The exclusions are \(x=2,-1\). Since \(2-x=-(x-2)\), cancellation leaves a factor of \(-1\).

\[\frac{(x+4)(x-2)}{(2-x)(x+1)}=-\frac{x+4}{x+1},\qquad x\ne2,-1\]

Decide whether \(x\) cancels in \(\frac{x+4}{x}\), and write an equivalent sum.

The numerator is a sum with no common factor \(x\). You can split it into two fractions.

\[\frac{x+4}{x}=1+\frac4x,\qquad x\ne0\]

Simplify \(\frac{6x^3-9x^2}{3x^2}\).

Factor out \(3x^2\) in the numerator. For \(x\ne0\), it cancels and leaves \(2x-3\).
