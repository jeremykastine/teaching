Lecture 16: Rational equations

Assessment coverage TSM 099 test 3

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to reveal or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

Start by recording the forbidden inputs. Simplify each side on the allowed domain and check every candidate in the original equation.

Key definitions

A rational equation contains rational expressions. An extraneous candidate fails the original equation, for instance because an original denominator is zero.

Solve \(\frac{x^2-1}{x-1}=4\).

Exclude \(x=1\). The left side simplifies to \(x+1\) for allowed inputs, so \(x=3\). In the original, \((9-1)/(3-1)=4\).

Solve \(\frac2{x-1}+\frac1{x+1}=\frac{8}{x^2-1}\).

Exclude \(x=\pm1\). Combine the left over \((x-1)(x+1)\) to get \((3x+1)/(x^2-1)\). Since the common denominator is nonzero, \(3x+1=8\) gives \(x=7/3\). Its original sides both equal \(9/5\).

Solve \(\frac{x}{x-2}=\frac3{x-2}+\frac1{x-2}\).

Exclude \(2\). Combine the right side as \(4/(x-2)\), then multiply both sides by \(x-2\). The solution is \(4\), which makes both original sides equal to \(2\).

Solve \(\frac{x+1}{x-1}=\frac6{x+2}\).

Exclude \(1,-2\). Moving the whole-side denominators gives \((x+1)(x+2)=6(x-1)\), or \(x^2-3x+8=0\). Its discriminant is \(-23\), so there are no real solutions.

Solve \(\frac{x}{x+1}=\frac2{x+3}\).

Exclude \(-1,-3\). Multiplication gives \(x(x+3)=2(x+1)\), or \((x+2)(x-1)=0\). Both candidates are allowed. At \(-2\) both sides equal \(2\), and at \(1\) both sides equal \(1/2\).

Solve \(\frac{x^2-4}{x-2}=4\) and explain any rejected candidate.

Exclude \(2\). The simplified equation \(x+2=4\) produces \(x=2\), which makes the original denominator zero. The solution set is empty.

Solve \(\frac{x^2-x}{x-1}=2+\frac{2}{x-1}\).

Exclude \(1\). The left side simplifies to \(x\) and the right becomes \(2x/(x-1)\). Then \(x(x-1)=2x\), so \(x(x-3)=0\). At \(0\) both original sides are \(0\), and at \(3\) both are \(3\).
