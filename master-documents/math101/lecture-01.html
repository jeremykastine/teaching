Lecture 1: Transformations of |x| + |y| = 1 and x2 + y2 = 1

Assessment coverage Test 1

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to reveal or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

A graph is the set of ordered pairs that satisfy its equation. For the diamond and circle, move the center and track the points furthest left, right, up, and down.

Key definitions

The domain is the set of x-coordinates attained. The range is the set of y-coordinates attained. A bracket includes an interval endpoint. In a parent equation, replacing \(x\) by \((x-h)/a\) and \(y\) by \((y-k)/b\), for positive \(a,b\), sends \((u,v)\) to \((h+au,k+bv)\).

Sketch \(|x|+|y|=1\) and \(x^2+y^2=1\). Give their centers, four extreme points, domains, and ranges.

Both are centered at \((0,0)\) and reach \((\pm1,0)\) and \((0,\pm1)\). The diamond joins those points with straight segments. The circle joins them with arcs. Each has domain and range \([-1,1]\).

Sketch \(|x-2|+|y+1|=1\). Give the center, extreme points, domain, and range.

Shift each point right \(2\) and down \(1\). The center is \((2,-1)\). The extremes are \((1,-1),(3,-1),(2,-2),(2,0)\). The domain is \([1,3]\) and the range is \([-2,0]\).

Sketch \(\left(\frac{x+1}{3}\right)^2+\left(\frac{y-2}{2}\right)^2=1\) by tracking the four extreme points of the unit circle.

Stretch horizontally by \(3\) and vertically by \(2\), then shift left \(1\) and up \(2\). The center is \((-1,2)\) and the extremes are \((-4,2),(2,2),(-1,0),(-1,4)\). The domain is \([-4,2]\) and the range is \([0,4]\).

Sketch \(\frac{|x|}{2}+\frac{|y-1|}{3}=1\). Give domain and range.

The center moves to \((0,1)\). The extreme points are \((-2,1),(2,1),(0,-2),(0,4)\). Join them with straight segments. The domain is \([-2,2]\) and the range is \([-2,4]\).

Write an equation for a circle centered at \((3,-2)\) with radius \(2\) by transforming the unit circle.

Stretch both axes by \(2\) and shift right \(3\) and down \(2\). Use \(\left(\frac{x-3}{2}\right)^2+\left(\frac{y+2}{2}\right)^2=1\). The extreme points are \(2\) units from the center along each axis.

Give the center, domain, and range of \((x+3)^2+(y-1)^2=1\).

The center is \((-3,1)\). Starting from the unit circle, shift left \(3\) and up \(1\). The horizontal interval is \([-4,-2]\) and the vertical interval is \([0,2]\).
