Lecture 19: Inverse Functions and Restricted Domains

Assessment coverage Test 4

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to reveal or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

An inverse reverses an input-output pair. A function needs distinct outputs for distinct inputs before this reversal is itself a function.

Key definitions

A one-to-one function passes the horizontal-line test. Its inverse \(f^{-1}\) swaps its domain and range. The graphs reflect across \(y=x\). For inverse pairs, each composition returns the original input on the appropriate domain.

Find and verify the inverse of \(f(x)=3x-5\).

Solve \(y=3x-5\) for the old input: \(x=(y+5)/3\). Name the new input \(x\), giving \(f^{-1}(x)=(x+5)/3\). The compositions are \(3((x+5)/3)-5=x\) and \(((3x-5)+5)/3=x\).

The domain of \(f\) is \(\{-1,0,2\}\) and its outputs there are \(4,7,1\), respectively. Give the inverse as ordered pairs, its domain, and its range.

Swap each pair. The inverse pairs are \((4,-1),(7,0),(1,2)\). The inverse domain is \(\{1,4,7\}\) and the range is \(\{-1,0,2\}\).

Explain why \(y=(x-2)^2+1\) on the entire real line has no inverse function.

The inputs \(1\) and \(3\) both give \(2\). Reversing the pairs would assign both \(1\) and \(3\) to input \(2\). On the graph the horizontal line \(y=2\) meets the parabola twice.

Find the inverse of \(f(x)=(x-2)^2+1\) for \(x\ge2\). Give both inverse domain and range.

The original range is \([1,\infty)\). Solve \(y-1=(x-2)^2\) using \(x-2\ge0\), so \(x=2+\sqrt{y-1}\). Therefore \(f^{-1}(x)=2+\sqrt{x-1}\), with domain \([1,\infty)\) and range \([2,\infty)\).

For \(f(x)=(x-2)^2+1\) restricted to \(x\le2\), find and verify the inverse.

Use the other branch: \(f^{-1}(x)=2-\sqrt{x-1}\) for \(x\ge1\). Its range is \((-\infty,2]\). For \(x\ge1\), \(f(f^{-1}(x))=(\sqrt{x-1})^2+1=x\). For \(x\le2\), \(f^{-1}(f(x))=2-|x-2|=x\).

The graph of a one-to-one function passes through \((-2,5)\) and \((3,1)\). Give two points of the inverse graph, and find \(f^{-1}(1)\).

The points swap to \((5,-2)\) and \((1,3)\). The inverse takes input \(1\) to output \(3\), so \(f^{-1}(1)=3\).
