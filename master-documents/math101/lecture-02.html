Lecture 2: Transformations of y = x

Assessment coverage Test 1

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to reveal or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

Track two points of a line after a change. Use the transformed points to read slope and intercepts from the sketch.

Key definitions

The slope is rise divided by run. An intercept is a point where the graph meets an axis. The vertical-line test checks whether each allowed \(x\) has exactly one corresponding \(y\).

Sketch the parent line \(y=x\) and its shift right by \(2\). Read both intercepts of the shifted line.

The points \((0,0)\) and \((1,1)\) move to \((2,0)\) and \((3,1)\). Continuing the line to the left gives the intercepts \((2,0)\) and \((0,-2)\). The slope is still \(1\).

Shift \(y=x\) down by \(2\). Compare its graph and equation with the graph obtained by shifting \(y=x\) right by \(2\).

A downward shift sends \((0,0)\) to \((0,-2)\) and \((1,1)\) to \((1,-1)\). Both shifts produce the same line \(y=x-2\). Moving right along a line of slope \(1\) places it at the same height as moving down by the same amount.

Sketch \(y=-2(x+1)+3\) by tracking \((0,0)\) and \((1,1)\) from \(y=x\). Read the slope and y-intercept.

Move left \(1\), multiply the original heights by \(-2\), and add \(3\). The points become \((-1,3)\) and \((0,1)\). The slope is \(-2\) and the y-intercept is \((0,1)\).

Sketch \(y=x/3\) by horizontally stretching \(y=x\). Give its slope, intercepts, domain, and range.

Multiply each original x-coordinate by \(3\). The points \((0,0),(1,1)\) become \((0,0),(3,1)\), giving slope \(1/3\). The axes meet the line at \((0,0)\). The domain and range are both \((-\infty,\infty)\).

Decide whether \(y=x-2\), \(|x|+|y|=1\), and \(x^2+y^2=1\) describe \(y\) as a function of \(x\). Use vertical lines.

Every vertical line meets \(y=x-2\) exactly once. At \(x=0\), each of the other graphs has the points \((0,1)\) and \((0,-1)\), so each gives two outputs at one input.

Algebra practice

Expand \(-2(3x-4)+x(2x+1)\) and collect like terms.

Distribute to every term. The four terms are \(-6x+8+2x^2+x\), giving \(2x^2-5x+8\).
