Topic 26: Solving Equations with Parentheses

Assigned to: TSM 098 and TSM 099

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to show or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

This topic solves linear equations containing parentheses by distributing first and then collecting and isolating terms.

Key definitions

Distributive property: multiplication of an outside factor by every term in parentheses. Like terms: terms with identical variable parts. Equivalent equation: an equation with the same solutions as the original.

Equations with grouped expressions

Distribute each outside factor to every term in its parentheses. A minus sign before a group acts as a factor of negative one. Then combine like terms and solve while keeping both sides equal. Decimal coefficients use the same algebra; multiplying the entire equation by a power of ten can clear decimals.

Solve

\[3x + 1 = - (x - 13).\]

The negative sign means multiplication by \(- 1\).

\[3x + 1 = - x + 13.\]

Add \(x\), subtract \(1\), and divide by \(4\).

\[4x = 12\]

\[x = 3.\]

Solve \(7(x+4)=9(x-2)\).

Distribute.

\[7x + 28 = 9x - 18.\]

Move the variable terms and constants.

\[46 = 2x\]

\[x = 23.\]

Solve \(-(4y+3)-(-3y-4)=0\).

In \(- (4y + 3) - ( - 3y - 4) = 0\), each leading subtraction changes both signs in the following parentheses.

\[- 4y - 3 + 3y + 4 = 0\]

\[- y + 1 = 0\]

\[y = 1.\]

A common error is to change only the first sign inside a subtracted group. Write the distributed line explicitly to prevent that mistake.

Solve \(0.30(12)+0.05x=0.10(18+x)\).

Multiply both sides by 100, then distribute and solve.

\[360+5x=10(18+x)\]

\[360+5x=180+10x\]

\[180=5x\]

\[x=36\]

Solve and check \(4(x-5)=2x+6\).

Use the same operation on both sides to isolate the variable.

\[4x-20=2x+6\quad\Rightarrow\quad2x=26\quad\Rightarrow\quad x=13\]

Check in the original equation (\(4(13-5)=32=2(13)+6\)).

Solve and check \(-2(3m-1)+5=4m-7\).

Use the same operation on both sides to isolate the variable.

\[-6m+7=4m-7\quad\Rightarrow\quad14=10m\quad\Rightarrow\quad m=\frac75\]

Check in the original equation (\(-2(3\cdot\frac75-1)+5=-\frac75=4\cdot\frac75-7\)).

Solve and check \(3-(2y-5)=2(y+1)\).

Use the same operation on both sides to isolate the variable.

\[8-2y=2y+2\quad\Rightarrow\quad6=4y\quad\Rightarrow\quad y=\frac32\]

Check in the original equation (\(3-(2\cdot\frac32-5)=5=2(\frac32+1)\)).

Distribute all three outside factors, then solve and check.

\[3(2x-1)-2(x+4)=5(x-3)+2\]

Distribute \(3\), \(-2\), and \(5\) in turn.

\[\begin{aligned} 6x-3-2(x+4)&=5(x-3)+2\\ 6x-3-2x-8&=5(x-3)+2\\ 6x-3-2x-8&=5x-15+2 \end{aligned}\]

Combine like terms and isolate the variable.

\[4x-11=5x-13\qquad\Rightarrow\qquad x=2\]

Check by substituting \(x=2\) in the original equation. The left side is \(3(4-1)-2(2+4)=-3\), and the right side is \(5(2-3)+2=-3\).

Solve and check. Begin by distributing inside the nested parentheses.

\[2\bigl(3(y-4)+1\bigr)=-4(y+2)+16\]

Distribute \(3\) inside the nested parentheses, then distribute \(2\) on the left and \(-4\) on the right.

\[\begin{aligned} 2(3y-12+1)&=-4(y+2)+16\\ 6y-24+2&=-4(y+2)+16\\ 6y-24+2&=-4y-8+16 \end{aligned}\]

Combine like terms and isolate the variable.

\[6y-22=-4y+8\qquad\Rightarrow\qquad 10y=30\qquad\Rightarrow\qquad y=3\]

Check by substituting \(y=3\) in the original equation. The left side is \(2(3(3-4)+1)=-4\), and the right side is \(-4(3+2)+16=-4\).
