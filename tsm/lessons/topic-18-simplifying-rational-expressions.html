Topic 18: Simplifying Rational Expressions

Assigned to: TSM 099

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to show or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

This topic simplifies rational expressions by factoring completely, canceling common factors, and preserving excluded input values.

Key definitions

Rational expression: a quotient of polynomials. Restriction: a value that makes an original denominator 0. Common factor: a factor appearing in both numerator and denominator. Cancellation: dividing numerator and denominator by the same nonzero factor.

Restrictions and cancellation

A rational expression is a ratio of polynomials with a nonzero denominator. Record excluded values from the original denominator before simplifying. Factor the numerator and denominator, then cancel only common nonzero factors, not separate terms in sums. Original restrictions remain after cancellation.

Simplify and state the restrictions from the original denominator.

\[\frac{6z - 30}{8z - 40}.\]

Factor the greatest common factor from the numerator and denominator.

\[\frac{6(z - 5)}{8(z - 5)}.\]

The original denominator is zero when \(z = 5\), so \(z \neq 5\). Cancel the common factor and reduce the numerical fraction.

\[\frac{6(z - 5)}{8(z - 5)} = \frac{6}{8} = \frac{3}{4},\quad\quad z \neq 5.\]

Simplify and state the restrictions from the original denominator.

\[\frac{x^{2} - 3x + 2}{x^{2} + 2x - 8}.\]

Factor both trinomials.

\[\frac{(x - 1)(x - 2)}{(x + 4)(x - 2)}.\]

The original denominator gives the restrictions \(x \neq - 4\) and \(x \neq 2\). Cancel the common factor \(x - 2\).

\[\frac{x - 1}{x + 4},\quad\quad x \neq - 4,2.\]

The canceled value \(x = 2\) does not become allowable. Simplifying changes the form of the expression, not its original domain.

Simplify and state the restrictions from the original denominator.

\[\frac{2k^2-2k}{7k^2-7}\]

\[\frac{2k^{2} - 2k}{7k^{2} - 7} = \frac{2k(k - 1)}{7(k - 1)(k + 1)} = \frac{2k}{7(k + 1)},\quad\quad k \neq - 1,1.\]

Simplify and state restrictions: \(\frac{12x^2-24x}{18x}\).

The original denominator requires \(x\ne0\). Factor and remove common nonzero factors.

\[\frac{12x^2-24x}{18x}=\frac{12x(x-2)}{18x}=\frac{2(x-2)}3\]

Simplify and state restrictions: \(\frac{a^2-16}{a^2+7a+12}\).

The original denominator requires \(a\ne-3,-4\). Factor and remove common nonzero factors.

\[\frac{a^2-16}{a^2+7a+12}=\frac{(a-4)(a+4)}{(a+3)(a+4)}=\frac{a-4}{a+3}\]

Simplify and state restrictions: \(\frac{3m^2+12m}{6m^2-24}\).

The original denominator requires \(m\ne-2,2\). Factor and remove common nonzero factors.

\[\frac{3m^2+12m}{6m^2-24}=\frac{3m(m+4)}{6(m-2)(m+2)}=\frac{m(m+4)}{2(m-2)(m+2)}\]
