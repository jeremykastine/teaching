Topic 18: Simplifying Rational Expressions

Assigned to: TSM 099

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to show or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

This topic simplifies rational expressions by factoring completely, canceling common factors, and preserving excluded input values.

Key definitions

Rational expression: a quotient of polynomials. Restriction: a value that makes an original denominator 0. Common factor: a factor appearing in both numerator and denominator. Cancellation: dividing numerator and denominator by the same nonzero factor.

Choose a factoring method first

For TSM 099, practice the full range of factoring used in Topics 14–17: remove a numerical or variable GCF, factor monic trinomials by sum and product (or guess and check), factor trinomials with leading coefficient greater than 1 by splitting the middle term, factor four terms by grouping, and recognize a difference of two squares. A single numerator or denominator may require more than one of these methods. Factor completely before looking for cancellations.

Opposite binomial factors differ by a negative sign: \(a-b=-(b-a)\). For example, \((x-5)/(5-x)=-1\) when \(x\ne5\). Keep the negative sign when canceling opposites. Never cancel individual terms across addition or subtraction: \((x+3)/x\) is not 3.

Restrictions and cancellation

A rational expression is a ratio of polynomials with a nonzero denominator. Record excluded values from the original denominator before simplifying. Factor the numerator and denominator, then cancel only common nonzero factors, not separate terms in sums. Original restrictions remain after cancellation.

Simplify and state the restrictions from the original denominator.

\[\frac{6z - 30}{8z - 40}.\]

Factor the greatest common factor from the numerator and denominator.

\[\frac{6(z - 5)}{8(z - 5)}.\]

The original denominator is zero when \(z = 5\), so \(z \neq 5\). Cancel the common factor and reduce the numerical fraction.

\[\frac{6(z - 5)}{8(z - 5)} = \frac{6}{8} = \frac{3}{4},\quad\quad z \neq 5.\]

Simplify and state the restrictions from the original denominator.

\[\frac{x^{2} - 3x + 2}{x^{2} + 2x - 8}.\]

Factor both trinomials.

\[\frac{(x - 1)(x - 2)}{(x + 4)(x - 2)}.\]

The original denominator gives the restrictions \(x \neq - 4\) and \(x \neq 2\). Cancel the common factor \(x - 2\).

\[\frac{x - 1}{x + 4},\quad\quad x \neq - 4,2.\]

The canceled value \(x = 2\) does not become allowable. Simplifying changes the form of the expression, not its original domain.

Simplify and state the restrictions from the original denominator.

\[\frac{2k^2-2k}{7k^2-7}\]

\[\frac{2k^{2} - 2k}{7k^{2} - 7} = \frac{2k(k - 1)}{7(k - 1)(k + 1)} = \frac{2k}{7(k + 1)},\quad\quad k \neq - 1,1.\]

Simplify and state restrictions: \(\frac{12x^2-24x}{18x}\).

The original denominator requires \(x\ne0\). Factor and remove common nonzero factors.

\[\frac{12x^2-24x}{18x}=\frac{12x(x-2)}{18x}=\frac{2(x-2)}3\]

Simplify and state restrictions: \(\frac{a^2-16}{a^2+7a+12}\).

The original denominator requires \(a\ne-3,-4\). Factor and remove common nonzero factors.

\[\frac{a^2-16}{a^2+7a+12}=\frac{(a-4)(a+4)}{(a+3)(a+4)}=\frac{a-4}{a+3}\]

Simplify and state restrictions: \(\frac{3m^2+12m}{6m^2-24}\).

The original denominator requires \(m\ne-2,2\). Factor and remove common nonzero factors.

\[\frac{3m^2+12m}{6m^2-24}=\frac{3m(m+4)}{6(m-2)(m+2)}=\frac{m(m+4)}{2(m-2)(m+2)}\]

More factoring combinations and opposite factors

Simplify using the sum-product method for a leading coefficient greater than 1. State restrictions.

\[\frac{6x^2+11x+3}{3x^2+7x+2}\]

In the numerator, split 11x into 9x+2x and group. In the denominator, split 7x into 6x+x and group.

\[\frac{3x(2x+3)+1(2x+3)}{3x(x+2)+1(x+2)} =\frac{(3x+1)(2x+3)}{(3x+1)(x+2)} =\frac{2x+3}{x+2}\]

The original denominator is zero at \(x=-\frac13,-2\); both values remain excluded.

Simplify by factoring a four-term polynomial by grouping and using a difference of squares. State restrictions.

\[\frac{x^3+3x^2-4x-12}{x^2-4}\]

Group the first two and last two numerator terms. Then factor the common binomial and the difference of squares.

\[\frac{x^2(x+3)-4(x+3)}{(x-2)(x+2)} =\frac{(x+3)(x^2-4)}{(x-2)(x+2)} =\frac{(x+3)(x-2)(x+2)}{(x-2)(x+2)} =x+3\]

Even though the simplified form is a polynomial, the original expression is undefined at \(x=-2,2\).

Simplify, being careful with opposite factors. State restrictions.

\[\frac{y^2-25}{5-y}\]

Factor the difference of squares. Rewrite the denominator as the negative of the matching factor.

\[\frac{(y-5)(y+5)}{-(y-5)} =-(y+5)=-y-5,\qquad y\ne5\]

The factor \(5-y\) is the opposite of \(y-5\); canceling them without the negative sign would give the wrong answer.

Simplify a trinomial together with opposite factors. State all original restrictions.

\[\frac{2t^2+3t-2}{(1-t)(t+2)}\]

Split the middle term: \(2t^2+4t-t-2=(t+2)(2t-1)\). The denominator has \(1-t=-(t-1)\), but here its factor \(t+2\) is the common one.

\[\frac{(t+2)(2t-1)}{(1-t)(t+2)} =\frac{2t-1}{1-t} =-\frac{2t-1}{t-1}\]

Original restrictions: \(t\ne1,-2\). The second form shows the negative sign required when the remaining denominator is reversed.

First remove the GCF, then factor a difference of squares and a monic trinomial. Simplify and state restrictions.

\[\frac{2u^2-8}{u^2+5u+6}\]

Both the numerical GCF and the difference of squares are needed in the numerator.

\[\frac{2(u^2-4)}{(u+2)(u+3)} =\frac{2(u-2)(u+2)}{(u+2)(u+3)} =\frac{2(u-2)}{u+3}\]

Original restrictions: \(u\ne-2,-3\), including the canceled denominator zero.

Determine whether anything cancels after factoring. Simplify and state restrictions.

\[\frac{v^2+v+1}{v^2-1}\]

The denominator is \((v-1)(v+1)\). The numerator has no integer factor pair with sum 1 and product 1, so it does not factor over the integers and shares no factor with the denominator.

\[\frac{v^2+v+1}{(v-1)(v+1)},\qquad v\ne-1,1\]

No cancellation is possible. Terms such as \(v^2\) cannot be canceled across sums.
