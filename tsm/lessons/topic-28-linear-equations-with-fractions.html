Topic 28: Linear Equations with Fractions

Assigned to: TSM 098 and TSM 099

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to show or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

This topic solves linear equations containing numerical fractions. Combining and simplifying each side makes the equation easier to solve.

Key definitions

Common denominator: a denominator shared by fractions so they can be added or subtracted. Multi-term numerator: a numerator containing a sum or difference.

Solve

\[\frac{x}{3}+\frac{x}{6}=5\]

Combine and simplify each side first. On the left, use a common denominator to add the fractions.

\[\frac{2x}{6}+\frac{x}{6}=5\]

\[\frac{3x}{6}=5\]

\[\frac{x}{2}=5\]

Move the denominator 2 to multiply the opposite side. Only the left side has a denominator to move.

\[x=2(5)\]

\[x=10\]

Moving a denominator is justified by multiplying both sides by that denominator. Here, multiplying both sides by 2 removes the division by 2 on the left.

Check in the original equation.

\[\frac{10}{3}+\frac{10}{6}=\frac{20}{6}+\frac{10}{6}=5\]

Solve

\[\frac{5}{4}x+\frac{1}{8}x=\frac{3}{8}+x\]

Combine each side into one fraction. Rewrite the left terms with denominator 8 and write the whole term on the right as a fraction with denominator 8.

\[\frac{10x}{8}+\frac{x}{8}=\frac{3}{8}+\frac{8x}{8}\]

\[\frac{11x}{8}=\frac{8x+3}{8}\]

Both sides have a denominator. Move both denominators to multiply the opposite sides in the same step. Each denominator multiplies the entire opposite numerator.

\[8(11x)=8(8x+3)\]

Divide both sides by 8, then solve.

\[11x=8x+3\]

\[3x=3\]

\[x=1\]

Check: both sides of the original equation equal \(11/8\) when \(x=1\).

Solve

\[\frac{x+7}{5}-\frac{19}{15}=\frac{x-3}{6}\]

Combine the fractions on the left, then distribute and combine like terms in the numerator.

\[\frac{3(x+7)-19}{15}=\frac{x-3}{6}\]

\[\frac{3x+21-19}{15}=\frac{x-3}{6}\]

\[\frac{3x+2}{15}=\frac{x-3}{6}\]

Move both denominators across in one step. Parentheses keep each numerator together.

\[6(3x+2)=15(x-3)\]

Divide both sides by 3 to keep the coefficients small, then distribute and solve.

\[2(3x+2)=5(x-3)\]

\[6x+4=5x-15\]

\[x=-19\]

Check in the original equation.

\[\frac{-19+7}{5}-\frac{19}{15}=\frac{-36-19}{15}=-\frac{11}{3}\]

\[\frac{-19-3}{6}=-\frac{11}{3}\]

Solve

\[\frac{x+2}{8}=\frac{x+3}{-5}\]

Each side is already one fraction. The negative sign belongs to the denominator, so keep it when moving both denominators to multiply the opposite sides.

\[-5(x+2)=8(x+3)\]

Distribute and solve.

\[-5x-10=8x+24\]

\[-13x=34\]

\[x=-\frac{34}{13}\]

Check: both sides of the original equation equal \(-1/13\).
