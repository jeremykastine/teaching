Topic 36: Word Problems with Percents

Assigned to: TSM 098 and TSM 099

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to show or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

This topic models real situations involving percents, including percent of a group, sales tax, total cost, and percent change.

Key definitions

Percent increase or decrease: change divided by the original amount. Sales tax: a percent of the pre-tax price. Total cost: original price plus tax or other added amounts.

Reading a percent problem

Identify the whole, the rate, and the part. Use the relationship \(\text{part}=\text{rate}\cdot\text{whole}\).

The unit attached to the whole should also be attached to the part.

A league has 65 players, and \(20\%\) are left-handed. How many players are left-handed?

A league has \(65\) players, and \(20\%\) are left-handed. Let \(p\) be the number of left-handed players.

\[p = 0.20(65) = 13.\]

There are \(13\) left-handed players.

A player made 286 field goals, approximately 46.9% of the attempts. Estimate the number of attempts, rounded to a whole number.

Let \(a\) be the number of attempts. The reported rate gives the approximate model \(286\approx0.469a\). Dividing gives \(a\approx\frac{286}{0.469}\approx609.8\), so the estimate is about 610 attempts. The percent was rounded, and attempts must be counted in whole numbers.

Find the sales tax and total cost of a $600 item taxed at 6%.

If an item costs \(\$ 600\) and the tax rate is \(6\%\), the tax is

\[0.06(600) = 36.\]

The total cost is \(600 + 36 = \$ 636\). The tax and the total are different requested quantities.

Percent change

Use the original value as the whole (\(\text{percent change}=\frac{|\text{new}-\text{original}|}{\text{original}}\cdot100\%\)).

Always state whether the change is an increase or a decrease.

A value decreases from 42.55 to 39.87. Find the percent decrease, rounded to the nearest tenth of a percent.

Divide the decrease by the original value, then convert to a percent (\(\frac{42.55-39.87}{42.55}\cdot100\%\approx6.3\%\)).

The value decreased, so the result is a 6.3% decrease.

Find 7% of 3.5 mL.

Convert the percent to a decimal and multiply by the whole.

\[0.07(3.5)=0.245\text{ mL}\]

Find the sales tax and total cost of a $245 purchase taxed at 8.2%.

The tax is the rate times the original price.

\[0.082(245)=20.09\]

The tax is $20.09; the total is \(245+20.09=265.09\) dollars.
