Topic 24: Solving Two-Term Equations by Dividing

Assigned to: TSM 098 and TSM 099

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to show or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

This topic solves short linear equations by undoing multiplication or division and checks the result in the original equation.

Key definitions

Linear equation: an equation whose variable has exponent 1. Inverse operations: operations that undo one another. Solution: a value that makes the original equation true.

Solve for x and check by substitution.

\[6x=42\]

The coefficient 6 multiplies x, so divide both sides by 6.

In a two-term equation such as \(ax = b\), the coefficient \(a\) multiplies the variable. Divide both sides by the same nonzero coefficient to preserve equality.

\[6x = 42\]

\[\frac{6x}{6} = \frac{42}{6}\]

\[x = 7.\]

Check: \(6(7)=42\), so the solution is correct.

Fractional solutions are ordinary solutions

\[16m = 15\]

Dividing both sides by \(16\) gives

\[m = \frac{15}{16}.\]

Do not change a correct fraction to a rounded decimal unless the problem requests an approximation.

When the variable is divided

If \(x/11 = - 23\), multiply both sides by \(11\).

\[11\left( \frac{x}{11} \right) = 11( - 23)\]

\[x = - 253.\]

Solve for z.

\[\frac{3}{5}z=\frac{9}{20}\]

Multiply both sides by the reciprocal \(\frac{5}{3}\).

\[z = \frac{5}{3} \cdot \frac{9}{20} = \frac{3}{4}.\]

The reciprocal works because \((5/3)(3/5) = 1\). Keep the equation balanced by multiplying both sides.

Solve and check: \(- 7x = 56\), \(1.8y = - 9\), \(q/14 = 3\), and \((4/9)t = - 10/27\).
