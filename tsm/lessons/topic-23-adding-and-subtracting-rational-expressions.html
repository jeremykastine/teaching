Topic 23: Adding and Subtracting Rational Expressions

Assigned to: TSM 099

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to show or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

This topic adds and subtracts rational expressions by factoring denominators, building the least common denominator, combining numerators, and simplifying.

Key definitions

Least common denominator (LCD): the least expression containing every denominator factor with sufficient multiplicity. Rational expression: a quotient of polynomials. Restriction: an input that makes an original denominator 0.

Add, simplify, and state all restrictions.

\[\frac{2}{3x}+\frac{5}{4x}\]

To add \(2/(3x)\) and \(5/(4x)\), the least common denominator is \(12x\).

\[\frac{2}{3x} + \frac{5}{4x} = \frac{8}{12x} + \frac{15}{12x} = \frac{23}{12x},\quad\quad x \neq 0.\]

Begin with the denominator

Rational expressions can be added or subtracted only after they have a common denominator. If the denominators already match, keep that denominator and combine the numerators.

\[\frac{7}{k} + \frac{5}{k} = \frac{12}{k},\quad\quad k \neq 0.\]

When subtracting, place the entire second numerator in parentheses so that the subtraction reaches every term.

Subtract, simplify, and state all restrictions.

\[\frac{x}{x + 2} - \frac{14}{x^{2} - 4}.\]

Factor the difference of squares: \(x^{2} - 4 = (x - 2)(x + 2)\). The LCD is \((x - 2)(x + 2)\), and the original restrictions are \(x \neq - 2,2\).

\[\frac{x(x - 2)}{(x + 2)(x - 2)} - \frac{14}{(x - 2)(x + 2)} = \frac{x^{2} - 2x - 14}{(x - 2)(x + 2)}.\]

The numerator does not factor over the integers, so no final cancellation is possible.

Simplify at the end when possible

\[\frac{3}{x - 1} - \frac{x + 5}{x^{2} - 1} = \frac{3(x + 1) - (x + 5)}{(x - 1)(x + 1)} = \frac{2x - 2}{(x - 1)(x + 1)} = \frac{2}{x + 1},\]

with \(x \neq - 1,1\). The final cancellation is valid only after the numerator has been combined and factored.

Simplify \(5/(2a) + 3/(6a)\), \(4/(x - 3) - 1/(x + 3)\), and \(2/(m + 4) + (m - 2)/\left( m^{2} - 16 \right)\). State all restrictions and check whether the final numerator factors.
