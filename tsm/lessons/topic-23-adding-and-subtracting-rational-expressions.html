Topic 23: Adding and Subtracting Rational Expressions

Assigned to: TSM 099

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to show or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

This topic adds and subtracts rational expressions by factoring denominators, building the least common denominator, combining numerators, and simplifying.

Key definitions

Least common denominator (LCD): the least expression containing every denominator factor with sufficient multiplicity. Rational expression: a quotient of polynomials. Restriction: an input that makes an original denominator 0.

Common denominators and restrictions

Record restrictions from the original denominators. Factor the denominators to find an LCD, then multiply each numerator and denominator by any missing factors. Combine the numerators over the LCD. For subtraction, subtract the entire second numerator. Finally factor and simplify the result, keeping the original restrictions.

Add, simplify, and state all restrictions.

\[\frac{2}{3x}+\frac{5}{4x}\]

To add \(2/(3x)\) and \(5/(4x)\), the least common denominator is \(12x\).

\[\frac{2}{3x} + \frac{5}{4x} = \frac{8}{12x} + \frac{15}{12x} = \frac{23}{12x},\quad\quad x \neq 0.\]

Add, simplify, and state all restrictions: \(\frac7k+\frac5k\).

The common denominator is k, which must be nonzero. Add the numerators.

\[\frac7k+\frac5k=\frac{12}k,\qquad k\ne0\]

Subtract, simplify, and state all restrictions.

\[\frac{x}{x + 2} - \frac{14}{x^{2} - 4}.\]

Factor the difference of squares: \(x^{2} - 4 = (x - 2)(x + 2)\). The LCD is \((x - 2)(x + 2)\), and the original restrictions are \(x \neq - 2,2\).

\[\frac{x(x - 2)}{(x + 2)(x - 2)} - \frac{14}{(x - 2)(x + 2)} = \frac{x^{2} - 2x - 14}{(x - 2)(x + 2)}.\]

The numerator does not factor over the integers, so no final cancellation is possible.

Subtract, simplify, and state all restrictions: \(\frac3{x-1}-\frac{x+5}{x^2-1}\).

\[\frac{3}{x - 1} - \frac{x + 5}{x^{2} - 1} = \frac{3(x + 1) - (x + 5)}{(x - 1)(x + 1)} = \frac{2x - 2}{(x - 1)(x + 1)} = \frac{2}{x + 1},\]

with \(x \neq - 1,1\). The final cancellation is valid only after the numerator has been combined and factored.

Simplify and state all restrictions: \(\frac5{2a}+\frac3{6a}\).

The original denominators require \(a\ne0\). Use the factored LCD, then combine numerators and check for common factors.

\[\frac{15+3}{6a}=\frac3a\]

The final numerator and denominator have no further common factor.

Simplify and state all restrictions: \(\frac4{x-3}-\frac1{x+3}\).

The original denominators require \(x\ne-3,3\). Use the factored LCD, then combine numerators and check for common factors.

\[\frac{4(x+3)-(x-3)}{(x-3)(x+3)}=\frac{3x+15}{(x-3)(x+3)}\]

The final numerator and denominator have no further common factor.

Simplify and state all restrictions: \(\frac2{m+4}+\frac{m-2}{m^2-16}\).

The original denominators require \(m\ne-4,4\). Use the factored LCD, then combine numerators and check for common factors.

\[\frac{2(m-4)+(m-2)}{(m-4)(m+4)}=\frac{3m-10}{(m-4)(m+4)}\]

The final numerator and denominator have no further common factor.
