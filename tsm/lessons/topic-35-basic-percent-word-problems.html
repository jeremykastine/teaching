Topic 35: Basic Percent Word Problems

Assigned to: TSM 098 and TSM 099

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to show or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

This topic translates basic percent questions into relationships among a part, a whole, and a percent rate.

Key definitions

Percent: a rate per 100. Part: the portion being compared. Whole: the total or reference amount. Percent equation: part = rate times whole, with the rate written as a decimal.

Percents and the percent equation

Percent means per hundred. Divide by 100 to convert a percent to a decimal; multiply by 100 to convert a decimal to a percent. Percents may exceed 100% or be less than 1%. In \(\text{part}=\text{rate}\cdot\text{whole}\), use a decimal rate. Decide which of the three quantities is unknown.

Compare and contrast these percent problems. Convert \(6.1\%\) to a decimal and \(0.007\) to a percent. Find \(13.3\%\) of 5200; find the number whose \(80\%\) is 20; and find what percent of 300 is 240.

The conversions are \(6.1\%=0.061\) and \(0.007=0.7\%\). For the other three problems, the unknown is respectively the part, the whole, or the rate:

\[p=0.133(5200)=691.6\]

\[20=0.80w\quad\Rightarrow\quad w=25\]

\[240=300r\quad\Rightarrow\quad r=0.80=80\%.\]
