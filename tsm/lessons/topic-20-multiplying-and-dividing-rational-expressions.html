Topic 20: Multiplying and Dividing Rational Expressions

Assigned to: TSM 099

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to show or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

This topic multiplies and divides rational expressions by factoring, using reciprocals for division, canceling common factors, and stating restrictions.

Key definitions

Rational expression: a quotient of polynomials. Reciprocal: an expression with numerator and denominator interchanged. Restriction: an input that makes an original denominator 0 or, in a division problem, makes the divisor 0.

Multiplying rational expressions

To multiply rational expressions, factor every numerator and denominator completely. Cancel common factors before multiplying. Early cancellation keeps the arithmetic and polynomial products small.

Look for a GCF first, then use trinomial factoring, grouping, or a difference of squares as needed. Some expressions need more than one method. Cancel whole factors, never separate terms in a sum. If all numerator factors cancel, their quotients multiply to 1: keep that 1 in the numerator.

Multiply, simplify, and state all restrictions.

\[\frac{6x}{x^{2} - 9} \cdot \frac{x + 3}{8x}.\]

Factor the difference of squares.

\[\frac{6x}{(x - 3)(x + 3)} \cdot \frac{x + 3}{8x}.\]

The original denominators require \(x \neq - 3,0,3\). Cancel \(x\), cancel \(x + 3\), and reduce \(6/8\).

\[\frac{3}{4(x - 3)},\quad\quad x \neq - 3,0,3.\]

Multiply, simplify, and state all restrictions.

\(\frac{15x^3}{8x}\cdot\frac{12x^2}{25x^2}\)

Both original denominators require \(x\ne0\). Multiply the numerator factors and denominator factors, then reduce the numerical coefficient and cancel the common powers of x.

\[\frac{15x^3}{8x}\cdot\frac{12x^2}{25x^2}=\frac{180x^5}{200x^3}=\frac{9x^2}{10},\qquad x\ne0\]

The original restriction remains after cancellation.

Simplify and state all restrictions: \(\frac{m-4}{m^2-16}\cdot\frac{2m+8}{5}\).

The original denominators must be nonzero, requiring \(m\ne-4,4\). Factor the difference of squares and take out the GCF 2, then cancel common factors.

\[\frac{m-4}{(m-4)(m+4)}\cdot\frac{2(m+4)}5=\frac25\]

Multiply, simplify, and state all restrictions.

\[\frac{3x+6}{x^2+5x+6}\cdot\frac{x+3}{3x+12}\]

Factor out the numerical GCFs. For the monic trinomial, 2 and 3 have product 6 and sum 5.

\[3x+6=3(x+2),\qquad x^2+5x+6=(x+2)(x+3),\qquad 3x+12=3(x+4)\]

The original denominators exclude \(x=-2,-3,-4\). Cancel the common factors 3, \(x+2\), and \(x+3\).

\[\frac{3(x+2)}{(x+2)(x+3)}\cdot\frac{x+3}{3(x+4)}=\frac{1}{x+4},\qquad x\ne-2,-3,-4\]

Every numerator factor divided by its matching denominator factor gives 1. Their product is 1, which stays in the numerator.

Multiply, simplify, and state all restrictions.

\[\frac{x^2-6x+9}{x^2-9}\cdot\frac{x+3}{x^2-3x}\]

The numerator is a perfect-square trinomial. The first denominator is a difference of squares, and the second has a variable GCF.

\[x^2-6x+9=(x-3)^2,\qquad x^2-9=(x-3)(x+3),\qquad x^2-3x=x(x-3)\]

The original denominators exclude \(x=0,3,-3\). Both copies of \(x-3\) in the numerator have matching copies in the denominator.

\[\frac{(x-3)^2}{(x-3)(x+3)}\cdot\frac{x+3}{x(x-3)}=\frac{1}{x},\qquad x\ne0,3,-3\]

Multiply, simplify, and state all restrictions.

\[\frac{6x^2+13x+6}{x^2+4x+4}\cdot\frac{x+2}{3x+2}\]

For the non-monic trinomial, the product is 36 and the sum is 13. Use 9 and 4, split the middle term, and group.

\[6x^2+9x+4x+6=3x(2x+3)+2(2x+3)=(3x+2)(2x+3)\]

The denominator \(x^2+4x+4=(x+2)^2\) is a perfect square. The original denominators exclude \(x=-2,-\frac23\).

\[\frac{(3x+2)(2x+3)}{(x+2)^2}\cdot\frac{x+2}{3x+2}=\frac{2x+3}{x+2},\qquad x\ne-2,-\frac23\]

Only one of the two denominator copies of \(x+2\) cancels.

Multiply, simplify, and state all restrictions.

\[\frac{x^3+2x^2-9x-18}{x^2+x-6}\cdot\frac{x-2}{x+3}\]

Group the four numerator terms, then factor the difference of squares that remains.

\[x^2(x+2)-9(x+2)=(x+2)(x^2-9)=(x+2)(x-3)(x+3)\]

The first denominator factors as \((x+3)(x-2)\). The original denominators exclude \(x=-3,2\).

\[\frac{(x+2)(x-3)(x+3)}{(x+3)(x-2)}\cdot\frac{x-2}{x+3}=\frac{(x+2)(x-3)}{x+3},\qquad x\ne-3,2\]

Dividing rational expressions

Keep the first rational expression, change division to multiplication, and flip the second rational expression. Only then factor and cancel.

Restrictions for division

Record restrictions from every original denominator. Also exclude values that make the divisor zero. Keep these restrictions after taking the reciprocal and canceling factors.

Divide, simplify, and state all restrictions.

\[\frac{z^2-9}{4z}\div\frac{z+3}{10}\]

\[\frac{z^{2} - 9}{4z} \div \frac{z + 3}{10} = \frac{z^{2} - 9}{4z} \cdot \frac{10}{z + 3}.\]

The restrictions include \(z \neq 0\) from the first denominator and \(z \neq - 3\) because the divisor cannot equal zero.

\[\frac{(z-3)(z+3)}{4z}\cdot\frac{10}{z+3}=\frac{5(z-3)}{2z},\qquad z\ne0,-3.\]

Divide, simplify, and state all restrictions: \(\frac{7-j}{10}\div\frac{j-7}{35}\).

The factors \(7 - j\) and \(j - 7\) are opposites (\(7 - j = - (j - 7)\)).

Recognizing this relationship prevents a lost negative sign. For example,

\[\frac{7 - j}{10} \div \frac{j - 7}{35} = \frac{7 - j}{10} \cdot \frac{35}{j - 7} = - \frac{7}{2},\quad\quad j \neq 7.\]

Simplify and state all restrictions: \(\frac{x^2-25}{3x}\div\frac{x+5}{9x^2}\).

Original denominators and any divisor must be nonzero, requiring \(x\ne0,-5\). Factor, use a reciprocal for division, and cancel common factors.

\[\frac{(x-5)(x+5)}{3x}\cdot\frac{9x^2}{x+5}=3x(x-5)\]

Divide, simplify, and state all restrictions.

\[\frac{2x^2-8x}{x^2-16}\div\frac{6x^2+6x}{x^2+5x+4}\]

Factor completely: take out the GCFs in both numerators, use a difference of squares, and factor the monic trinomial.

\[2x^2-8x=2x(x-4),\quad x^2-16=(x-4)(x+4)\]

\[6x^2+6x=6x(x+1),\quad x^2+5x+4=(x+1)(x+4)\]

The original denominators exclude \(x=4,-4,-1\). The divisor is zero at \(x=0\), so exclude 0 as well. Keep the first expression and multiply by the reciprocal of the second.

\[\frac{2x(x-4)}{(x-4)(x+4)}\cdot\frac{(x+1)(x+4)}{6x(x+1)}=\frac13,\qquad x\ne4,-4,-1,0\]

All variable factors cancel. Reducing 2 over 6 leaves 1 over 3; the numerator 1 remains.

Divide, simplify, and state all restrictions.

\[\frac{6x^2-x-2}{x^2-4}\div\frac{3x-2}{x-2}\]

For the non-monic trinomial, the product is -12 and the sum is -1. Split the middle term using -4 and 3, then group.

\[6x^2-4x+3x-2=2x(3x-2)+(3x-2)=(2x+1)(3x-2)\]

The original denominators exclude \(x=2,-2\); the divisor is zero at \(x=\frac23\). Factor the difference of squares and use the reciprocal.

\[\frac{(2x+1)(3x-2)}{(x-2)(x+2)}\cdot\frac{x-2}{3x-2}=\frac{2x+1}{x+2},\qquad x\ne2,-2,\frac23\]

Divide, simplify, and state all restrictions.

\[\frac{x^2-4x+4}{4-x^2}\div\frac{x-2}{x^2+4x+4}\]

The trinomials are perfect squares, and the first denominator is a difference of squares with opposite factors.

\[x^2-4x+4=(x-2)^2,\quad 4-x^2=(2-x)(2+x)=-(x-2)(x+2)\]

\[x^2+4x+4=(x+2)^2\]

The original denominators exclude \(x=2,-2\). The divisor would be zero at 2, which is already excluded. Use the reciprocal and retain the negative sign from the opposite factors.

\[\frac{(x-2)^2}{-(x-2)(x+2)}\cdot\frac{(x+2)^2}{x-2}=-(x+2),\qquad x\ne2,-2\]
