Topic 20: Multiplying and Dividing Rational Expressions

Assigned to: TSM 099

Double-click or double-tap the space beneath a problem to show or hide its solution. With a keyboard, focus that space and press Enter or Space.

Overview

This topic multiplies and divides rational expressions by factoring, using reciprocals for division, canceling common factors, and stating restrictions.

Key definitions

Rational expression: a quotient of polynomials. Reciprocal: an expression with numerator and denominator interchanged. Restriction: an input that makes any original denominator 0.

Multiply, simplify, and state all restrictions.

\[\frac{6x}{x^{2} - 9} \cdot \frac{x + 3}{8x}.\]

To multiply rational expressions, factor every numerator and denominator completely. Cancel common factors before multiplying. Early cancellation keeps the arithmetic and polynomial products small.

Factor the difference of squares.

\[\frac{6x}{(x - 3)(x + 3)} \cdot \frac{x + 3}{8x}.\]

The original denominators require \(x \neq - 3,0,3\). Cancel \(x\), cancel \(x + 3\), and reduce \(6/8\).

\[\frac{3}{4(x - 3)},\quad\quad x \neq - 3,0,3.\]

Divide, simplify, and state all restrictions.

\[\frac{z^2-9}{4z}\div\frac{z+3}{10}\]

Keep the first rational expression, change division to multiplication, and flip the second rational expression. Only then factor and cancel.

\[\frac{z^{2} - 9}{4z} \div \frac{z + 3}{10} = \frac{z^{2} - 9}{4z} \cdot \frac{10}{z + 3}.\]

The restrictions include \(z \neq 0\) from the first denominator and \(z \neq - 3\) because the divisor cannot equal zero.

\[\frac{(z-3)(z+3)}{4z}\cdot\frac{10}{z+3}=\frac{5(z-3)}{2z},\qquad z\ne0,-3.\]

Opposite factors and signs

The factors \(7 - j\) and \(j - 7\) are opposites:

\[7 - j = - (j - 7).\]

Recognizing this relationship prevents a lost negative sign. For example,

\[\frac{7 - j}{10} \div \frac{j - 7}{35} = \frac{7 - j}{10} \cdot \frac{35}{j - 7} = - \frac{7}{2},\quad\quad j \neq 7.\]

Simplify \(\left( 15a^{2} \right)/(8b) \cdot \left( 12b^{2} \right)/(25a)\), \(\left( x^{2} - 25 \right)/(3x) \div \left( (x + 5)/\left( 9x^{2} \right) \right)\), and \((m - 4)/\left( m^{2} - 16 \right) \cdot (2m + 8)/5\). State all restrictions.
