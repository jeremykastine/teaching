Every lesson is labeled by course. During a TSM 099-only lesson, TSM 098 students review the listed topics and open the practice PDF beside that lesson. The current sequence has no TSM 098-only lessons.

Each practice packet has three core pages and one extension page, followed by answers on separate pages. Plan roughly 45–60 minutes for core work and 15–25 minutes for extensions, depending on pace. Show all work, check the answers at the end after attempting a section, and correct mistakes.
