TSM 098 and TSM 099

Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What does percent mean?
  2. How do you convert a percent to a decimal?
  3. What equation relates part, rate, and whole?
  4. How do you find what percent one quantity is of another?
  5. Which quantity is the base in a percent problem?
Check short-answer key
  1. Per hundred.
  2. Divide by 100.
  3. Part = rate × whole, with rate in decimal form.
  4. Divide part by whole, then multiply by 100%.
  5. The whole to which the percentage applies.

Example problems

Example 1

Find 18% of 250.

Show solution
  1. Convert 18% to 0.18.
  2. Multiply: 0.18 × 250 = 45.

Example 2

Thirty is what percent of 120?

Show solution
  1. Divide part by whole: 30/120 = 0.25.
  2. Convert to percent: 25%.
