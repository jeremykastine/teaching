Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What are terms?
  2. What makes terms like terms?
  3. What is a coefficient?
  4. How do you combine like terms?
  5. Are x and x squared like terms?
Check short-answer key
  1. Parts of a sum separated by addition or subtraction, with their signs.
  2. They have exactly the same variable factors and exponents.
  3. The numerical factor multiplying a variable part.
  4. Add or subtract coefficients and keep the variable part.
  5. No; their exponents differ.

Example problems

Example 1

Simplify 4x−7+3x+2.

Show solution
  1. Combine x terms: 4x + 3x = 7x.
  2. Combine constants: −7 + 2 = −5.
  3. Result: 7x − 5.

Example 2

Simplify 5x2−2x+3x2+6x.

Show solution
  1. Combine squared terms: 5x2+3x2=8x2.
  2. Combine linear terms: −2x + 6x = 4x.
  3. Result: 8x2+4x.
