Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What is a solution of a system?
  2. How are solutions located graphically?
  3. What do distinct parallel lines mean?
  4. What do coincident lines mean?
  5. How do you verify a visually estimated intersection?
Check short-answer key
  1. An ordered pair satisfying every equation in the system.
  2. At intersections of the graphs.
  3. No solution.
  4. Infinitely many solutions.
  5. Substitute its coordinates into both equations.

Example problems

Example 1

Solve y = x + 1 and y = −x + 5 by graphing.

Show solution
  1. Plot (0, 1) and (2, 3) for the first line.
  2. Plot (0, 5) and (2, 3) for the second.
  3. They intersect at (2, 3); 3 = 2 + 1 and 3 = −2 + 5 verify it.

Example 2

Classify y = 2x + 1 and y = 2x − 3.

Show solution
  1. Both have slope 2 and different intercepts.
  2. Their graphs are distinct parallel lines and never intersect.
  3. The system has no solution.
