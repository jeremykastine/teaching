Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. State the distributive property.
  2. What must happen to every term inside parentheses?
  3. How do you distribute a minus sign?
  4. What should follow distribution when possible?
  5. What operation reverses distribution?
Check short-answer key
  1. a(b+c)=ab+ac.
  2. Each term is multiplied by the outside factor.
  3. Multiply every term inside by −1.
  4. Combine like terms.
  5. Factoring.

Example problems

Example 1

Expand −3(2x − 5).

Show solution
  1. Multiply −3 by 2x to get −6x.
  2. Multiply −3 by −5 to get +15.
  3. Result: −6x + 15.

Example 2

Simplify 2(x + 4) − (3x − 1).

Show solution
  1. Distribute to get 2x + 8 − 3x + 1.
  2. Combine like terms: −x + 9.
