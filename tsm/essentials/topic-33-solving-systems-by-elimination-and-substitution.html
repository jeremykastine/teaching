Example problems

Example 1

Solve y = 2x − 1 and x + y = 8 by substitution.

Show solution
  1. Substitute 2x − 1 for y: x + 2x − 1 = 8.
  2. Thus 3x = 9 and x = 3.
  3. Then y = 5; (3, 5) satisfies both equations.

Example 2

Solve x + y = 7 and x − y = 1 by elimination.

Show solution
  1. Add the equations: 2x = 8.
  2. Thus x = 4; substitute into x + y = 7 to get y = 3.
  3. Check: 4 + 3 = 7 and 4 − 3 = 1.
