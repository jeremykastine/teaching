Example problems

Example 1

Factor 6x2+11x+3.

Show solution
  1. ac = 18; 9 and 2 have product 18 and sum 11.
  2. Rewrite as 6x2+9x+2x+3.
  3. Group: 3x(2x + 3) + 1(2x + 3).
  4. Result: (3x + 1)(2x + 3).

Example 2

Factor 4x2−4x−3.

Show solution
  1. ac = −12; −6 and 2 sum to −4.
  2. Rewrite as 4x2−6x+2x−3.
  3. Group: 2x(2x − 3) + 1(2x − 3).
  4. Result: (2x + 1)(2x − 3).
