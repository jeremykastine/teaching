Topic 13: Greatest Common Divisor (GCD) · Essentials

TSM 098 and TSM 099

Answer the questions from memory on paper. Then check the answer key. Attempt each example before opening its solution.

Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What is the greatest common divisor?
  2. How can lists of divisors find a GCD?
  3. How do prime factorizations give a GCD?
  4. What does relatively prime mean?
  5. How do you find the common variable factor in xm and xn?
Check short-answer key
  1. The largest positive integer dividing each given integer.
  2. List positive divisors of each number and take the largest shared one.
  3. Use only shared primes, with their smaller exponents.
  4. The GCD is 1.
  5. Use x raised to the smaller exponent.

Example problems

Example 1

Find GCD(18, 24) by both methods.

Show solution
  1. Divisors of 18: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18; of 24: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24. Largest shared: 6.
  2. Prime factors: 18=2·32, 24=23·3. Shared minimum powers give 2 × 3 = 6.

Example 2

Find the GCF of 18x3 and 24x2.

Show solution
  1. The numerical GCD is 6.
  2. The smaller x exponent is 2.
  3. The GCF is 6x2.
