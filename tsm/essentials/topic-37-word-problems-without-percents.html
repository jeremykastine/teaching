Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

Check short-answer key
  1. The quantity it represents and its units.
  2. x + 5.
  3. x − 5.
  4. Units and a check that the answer makes sense in context.
  5. Distance = rate × time.

Example problems

Example 1

Three more than twice a number is 17. Find the number.

Show solution
  1. Let x be the number; 2x + 3 = 17.
  2. Subtract 3 and divide by 2: x = 7.
  3. Check: 2(7) + 3 = 17.

Example 2

A car travels at a constant 45 miles per hour for 2.5 hours. Find the distance.

Show solution
  1. Use distance = rate × time.
  2. 45 × 2.5 = 112.5 miles.
  3. The hour units cancel, leaving miles.
