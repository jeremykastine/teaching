Example problems

Example 1

Simplify 2x−1+3x+1.

Show solution
  1. Require x ≠ ±1; the LCD is (x − 1)(x + 1).
  2. The numerator is 2(x + 1) + 3(x − 1) = 5x − 1.
  3. Result: 5x−1(x−1)(x+1).

Example 2

Simplify xx−2−2x−2.

Show solution
  1. Require x ≠ 2.
  2. Subtract numerators to obtain (x − 2)/(x − 2).
  3. Cancel the common factor: 1, for x ≠ 2.
