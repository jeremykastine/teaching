Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What factor should you look for when simplifying a square root?
  2. For nonnegative a and b, how can ab be separated?
  3. When is a numerical square root fully simplified?
  4. Can a+b generally be split into a+b?
  5. How do you combine like radicals?
Check short-answer key
  1. A perfect-square factor greater than 1.
  2. As ab.
  3. No perfect-square factor greater than 1 remains inside.
  4. No.
  5. Add their coefficients while keeping the common radical.

Example problems

Example 1

Simplify 72.

Show solution
  1. 72 = 36 × 2.
  2. 72=362=62.

Example 2

Simplify 312−27.

Show solution
  1. 12=23 and 27=33.
  2. Substitute: 63−33=33.
