Topic 36: Word Problems with Percents · Essentials

TSM 098 and TSM 099

Answer the questions from memory on paper. Then check the answer key. Attempt each example before opening its solution.

Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. How do you calculate a percent increase?
  2. How do you calculate a percent decrease?
  3. What denominator is used for percent change?
  4. Do a 20% increase and a 20% decrease cancel?
  5. How do you recover an original price from a discounted price?
Check short-answer key
  1. Multiply the original quantity by 1 plus the decimal rate.
  2. Multiply the original quantity by 1 minus the decimal rate.
  3. The original quantity.
  4. No; the second percent uses a different base.
  5. Divide by the remaining fraction of the original price.

Example problems

Example 1

An $80 item is discounted 25%. Find its sale price.

Show solution
  1. The remaining fraction is 1 − 0.25 = 0.75.
  2. Sale price = 80 × 0.75 = $60.

Example 2

A price after 10% tax is $55. Find the price before tax.

Show solution
  1. Let p be the original price; 1.10p = 55.
  2. Divide: p = 55/1.10 = $50.
  3. Check: $50 plus $5 tax is $55.
