Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What does a positive integer exponent count?
  2. Why do parentheses matter in a negative base?
  3. What does the principal square root mean?
  4. What is a perfect square?
  5. What is a0 when a is nonzero?
Check short-answer key
  1. The number of equal factors of the base.
  2. They determine whether the negative sign is included in the repeated factor.
  3. The nonnegative number whose square equals the radicand.
  4. A number equal to an integer squared.
  5. 1.

Example problems

Example 1

Evaluate −34 and (−3)4.

Show solution
  1. Exponents precede a leading negative: −34=−(81)=−81.
  2. With parentheses, four negative factors give (−3)4=81.

Example 2

Evaluate 144, −49, and 25.

Show solution
  1. The nonnegative square root of 144 is 12.
  2. The negative sign stays outside: −49=−7.
  3. Five factors of 2 give 25=32.
