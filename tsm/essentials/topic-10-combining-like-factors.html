Example problems

Example 1

Simplify (3x2)(−2x4).

Show solution
  1. Multiply coefficients: 3(−2) = −6.
  2. Add exponents on x: 2 + 4 = 6.
  3. Result: −6x6.

Example 2

Simplify (−2x3)2.

Show solution
  1. Square both factors: (−2)2(x3)2.
  2. The coefficient is 4 and the exponent is 3 × 2 = 6.
  3. Result: 4x6.
