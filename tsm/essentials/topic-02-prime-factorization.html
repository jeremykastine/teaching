Example problems

Example 1

Find the prime factorization of 84.

Show solution
  1. 84 = 2 × 42 = 2 × 2 × 21.
  2. 21 = 3 × 7, so 84=22·3·7.
  3. Check: 4 × 3 × 7 = 84.

Example 2

Find the prime factorization of 90.

Show solution
  1. 90 = 9 × 10.
  2. Factor each part: 9 = 3 × 3 and 10 = 2 × 5.
  3. Thus 90=2·32·5.
