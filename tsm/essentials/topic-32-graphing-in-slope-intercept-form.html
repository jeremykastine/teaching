Topic 32: Graphing in Slope-Intercept Form · Essentials

TSM 099 only

Answer the questions from memory on paper. Then check the answer key. Attempt each example before opening its solution.

Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What is slope-intercept form?
  2. What does m represent?
  3. What point is identified by b?
  4. What does a negative slope indicate?
  5. Can a vertical line be written as y = mx + b?
Check short-answer key
  1. y = mx + b.
  2. Slope: vertical change divided by horizontal change.
  3. The y-intercept (0, b).
  4. The line falls as you move right.
  5. No; its slope is undefined.

Example problems

Example 1

Graph y = −2x + 3.

Show solution
  1. Start at the y-intercept (0, 3).
  2. Slope −2 = −2/1 means down 2 and right 1 to (1, 1).
  3. Draw the line through these points; (2, −1) also lies on it.

Example 2

Rewrite 3x + 2y = 6 and identify slope and intercept.

Show solution
  1. Subtract 3x: 2y = −3x + 6.
  2. Divide by 2: y = −(3/2)x + 3.
  3. The slope is −3/2 and the y-intercept is (0, 3).
