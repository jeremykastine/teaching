Topic 4: Simplifying Fractions · Essentials

TSM 098 and TSM 099

Answer the questions from memory on paper. Then check the answer key. Attempt each example before opening its solution.

Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What makes two fractions equivalent?
  2. What operation preserves a fraction's value?
  3. What does simplest form mean?
  4. Why can you not cancel a number appearing in only one term of a sum?
  5. What must be true of a denominator?
Check short-answer key
  1. They represent the same number.
  2. Multiply or divide numerator and denominator by the same nonzero number.
  3. The numerator and denominator have no common factor greater than 1.
  4. Cancellation divides entire numerator and denominator by a common factor.
  5. It must not equal zero.

Example problems

Example 1

Simplify 42/60.

Show solution
  1. The greatest common divisor of 42 and 60 is 6.
  2. Divide both by 6: 42/60 = 7/10.
  3. 7 and 10 share no factor greater than 1.

Example 2

Simplify −18/24 and 0/9.

Show solution
  1. Divide −18 and 24 by 6: −18/24 = −3/4.
  2. Zero divided by a nonzero number equals zero, so 0/9 = 0.
