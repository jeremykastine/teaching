Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What does factoring out a GCF accomplish?
  2. How is each term inside the parentheses found?

  4. How do you check a factorization?
  5. What must you check after factoring once?
Check short-answer key
  1. It rewrites a sum as a product of the GCF and a remaining polynomial.
  2. Divide its original term by the GCF.
  3. 1.
  4. Distribute to recover the original expression.
  5. Whether any common factor remains in all terms inside.

Example problems

Example 1

Factor 12x3−18x2.

Show solution
  1. The GCF is 6x2.
  2. Divide each term by it: 2x and −3.
  3. Result: 6x2(2x−3); distribution recovers the original.

Example 2

Factor 7x + 7.

Show solution
  1. The GCF is 7.
  2. Dividing both terms by 7 gives x and 1.
  3. Result: 7(x + 1).
