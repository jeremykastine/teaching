Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. How do you multiply fractions?
  2. What is a reciprocal?
  3. How do you divide by a fraction?
  4. Which fraction is inverted in a division?
  5. What remains when the numerator cancels completely?
Check short-answer key
  1. Multiply numerators and multiply denominators, then simplify.
  2. The number which multiplies a given nonzero number to give 1.
  3. Multiply by its reciprocal.
  4. The divisor, the second fraction.
  5. 1, not 0.

Example problems

Example 1

Compute 23·38.

Show solution
  1. Cancel the common 3 and reduce 2/8.
  2. The product is 1/4; the remaining numerator is 1.

Example 2

Compute 512÷109.

Show solution
  1. Multiply by the reciprocal: 512·910.
  2. Reduce 5/10 to 1/2 and 9/12 to 3/4.
  3. The result is 3/8.
