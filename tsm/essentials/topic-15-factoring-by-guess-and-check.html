Topic 15: Factoring by Guess and Check · Essentials

TSM 098 and TSM 099

Answer the questions from memory on paper. Then check the answer key. Attempt each example before opening its solution.

Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. For (mx+p)(nx+q), what determines the leading coefficient?
  2. What determines the constant term?
  3. What determines the middle coefficient?
  4. What sign pattern gives a negative constant?
  5. What should you do if no integer factor pair works?
Check short-answer key
  1. The product mn.
  2. The product pq.
  3. The cross-term sum mq + np.
  4. Opposite signs on the constant factors.
  5. State that the polynomial is not factorable into integer linear factors.

Example problems

Example 1

Factor 2x2+7x+3.

Show solution
  1. Try first terms 2x and x, and last terms 1 and 3.
  2. (2x + 1)(x + 3) gives cross terms 6x + x = 7x.
  3. Thus the factorization is (2x + 1)(x + 3).

Example 2

Factor x2−25, and test 2x2+x+2 for integer factors.

Show solution
  1. The difference of squares gives x2−25=(x−5)(x+5).
  2. For the second, first factors must be 2x and x; constant factors must both be 1 and 2, or both negative.
  3. Possible middle coefficients are ±4 or ±5, never 1, so there are no integer linear factors.
