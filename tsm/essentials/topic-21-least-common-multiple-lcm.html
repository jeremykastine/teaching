Topic 21: Least Common Multiple (LCM) · Essentials

TSM 098 and TSM 099

Answer the questions from memory on paper. Then check the answer key. Attempt each example before opening its solution.

Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What is the least common multiple?
  2. How do prime factorizations give an LCM?
  3. How does an LCM differ from a GCD?
  4. What is a least common denominator?
  5. Must the LCM equal the product?
Check short-answer key
  1. The smallest positive integer that is a multiple of every given positive integer.
  2. Use every prime appearing, with its largest exponent.
  3. An LCM is a shared multiple; a GCD is a shared divisor.
  4. The LCM of the denominators.
  5. No; shared factors can make it smaller.

Example problems

Example 1

Find LCM(12, 18).

Show solution
  1. 12=22·3 and 18=2·32.
  2. Take largest powers: 22·32=36.

Example 2

Find the LCD of 1/8 and 5/12.

Show solution
  1. 8=23 and 12=22·3.
  2. The LCD is 23·3=24.
  3. The equivalent fractions are 3/24 and 10/24.
