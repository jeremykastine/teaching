Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. How can you remove parentheses in a linear equation?
  2. What happens when a negative multiplies a parenthesis?
  3. What follows distribution?
  4. Should you distribute to only the first term?
  5. Where should a proposed solution be checked?
Check short-answer key
  1. Distribute each outside factor to every term inside.
  2. Every enclosed term changes sign and magnitude according to that factor.
  3. Combine like terms, collect variables, and isolate the variable.
  4. No; distribute to every term.
  5. In the original equation.

Example problems

Example 1

Solve 3(x − 2) + 4 = 2x + 7.

Show solution
  1. Distribute and combine: 3x − 2 = 2x + 7.
  2. Subtract 2x and add 2: x = 9.
  3. Check: both sides are 25.

Example 2

Solve 5 − 2(x + 3) = 7.

Show solution
  1. Distribute: 5 − 2x − 6 = 7.
  2. Combine: −2x − 1 = 7, so −2x = 8 and x = −4.
  3. Check: 5 − 2(−1) = 7.
