Example problems

Example 1

Expand (x + 3)(x − 5).

Show solution
  1. Multiply all four pairs: x2−5x+3x−15.
  2. Combine linear terms: x2−2x−15.

Example 2

Expand (2x−1)(x2+3x+4).

Show solution
  1. Distribute 2x: 2x3+6x2+8x.
  2. Distribute −1: −x2−3x−4.
  3. Combine: 2x3+5x2+5x−4.
