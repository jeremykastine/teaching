# TSM

Lecture notes and planning documents for the co-listed TSM 098–099 course.

- `../index.html` is the single Teaching page with direct links to lecture notes, homework, and practice tests under TSM 098 / 099. `tsm/index.html` redirects there.
- `lecture-notes.html` is the table of contents for the browser edition.
- `lessons/` contains one HTML file for each of the 37 topics.
- `planning/` contains the co-listed course schedule.
- `specifications/` contains the lecture-note and test specifications.

The HTML notes are the maintained lecture-note source and include 373 worked solutions revealed or hidden by double-clicking or double-tapping the solution area (or focusing it and pressing Enter/Space). Each concealed solution uses white text on a white background and preserves its natural height, providing a work area sized to the amount of solution content. The complete solution text stays in the HTML and rendered document for text extraction and assistive reading; revealing it changes only its formatting. Mathematical notation is rendered with MathJax 4, with inline and display formulas wrapping to the available width and no formula scrollbars or vertical clipping. Topic 5, Evaluating Powers and Square Roots, contains nine separately revealed worked examples: a powers-of-three table, mixed positive-base practice, a two-column negative-sign comparison table, and mixed signed-power practice, followed by the five square-root examples. Topic 1 teaches division notation and combining/separating successive divisions alongside divisibility, without introducing unit-fraction multiplication. Topics 7 and 8 are unified lessons: everyone studies every problem, and selected problems carry the label **Assessment: TSM 099 only**. Topic 7 retains all 16 evaluation examples, including the quadratic example required by the TSM 098 Arithmetic Test. Topic 8 contains 12 paired-expression comparisons covering agreement throughout a common domain, occasional agreement, and impossibility of agreement, with attention to restrictions and the limits of numerical evidence. All divisions in Topic 7 produce integers. Topic 2 introduces exponent notation briefly for prime factorization; full power instruction follows signed numbers and fractions in Topic 5. Established topic numbers and lesson URLs are retained.

Edit lecture notes directly in `lessons/`. All lecture-note content is maintained in HTML; no parallel Word documents are kept.

## Homework

Compact PDF homework assignments are linked from `homework.html`. See [the homework design and coverage map](homework/README.md) for priorities, topic groupings, editable sources, and rebuild instructions. Students work on separate paper and upload one best representative page in Blackboard.
