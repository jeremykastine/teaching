Example problems

Example 1

Of 40 students, 10 take tutoring and pass, 5 take tutoring and fail, 20 skip tutoring and pass, and 5 skip tutoring and fail. Find P(pass) and P(tutoring and pass).

Show solution
  1. Passing total = 10 + 20 = 30, so P(pass) = 30/40 = 3/4.
  2. The joint count is 10, so P(tutoring and pass) = 10/40 = 1/4.
