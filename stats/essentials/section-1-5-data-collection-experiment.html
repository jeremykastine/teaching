Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What is an operational definition?
  2. Why record units with measurements?
  3. Why use the same collection procedure for all participants?
  4. What should be done with missing responses?
  5. Why protect participants' identities?
Check short-answer key
  1. A precise statement of how a variable will be measured.
  2. Without units, recorded values may be ambiguous or incomparable.
  3. To reduce differences caused by measurement procedures.
  4. Record them as missing and report how they are handled; do not silently treat them as zero.
  5. To respect privacy and reduce unnecessary disclosure.

Example problems

Example 1

Plan a small study of student commute times.

Show solution
  1. Define commute time as minutes from leaving home to arriving on campus for one specified day.
  2. Use the same question and units for everyone; record missing responses separately.
  3. Obtain appropriate consent and avoid collecting names unless necessary.

Example 2

A dataset records 12, 18, blank, and 30 minutes. Find the mean of recorded times and report missingness.

Show solution
  1. Three times were recorded and one is missing.
  2. Mean of recorded times = (12 + 18 + 30)/3 = 20 minutes.
  3. Dividing by 4 would incorrectly treat the blank as zero; report the missing response.
