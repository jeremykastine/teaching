Statistics

5.3: The Exponential Distribution · Essentials

Answer the questions from memory on paper. Then check the answer key. Attempt each example before opening its solution.

Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What does the exponential model commonly measure?
  2. Using rate λ, what is its mean?
  3. Using rate λ, what is P(X > t) for t ≥ 0?
  4. What is its standard deviation?
  5. What is the memoryless property?
Check short-answer key
  1. Waiting time to the next event in a constant-rate Poisson process.
  2. 1/λ.
  3. e−λt.
  4. 1/λ, equal to its mean.
  5. P(X > s + t | X > s) = P(X > t), for nonnegative s and t.

Example problems

Example 1

A waiting time is exponential with mean 5 minutes. Find P(X > 10).

Show solution
  1. Rate λ = 1/5 per minute.
  2. P(X > 10) = e⁻² ≈ 0.1353.

Example 2

With rate 0.5 per minute, find the probability of waiting at most 2 minutes.

Show solution
  1. P(X ≤ 2) = 1 − P(X > 2).
  2. This equals 1 − e⁻¹ ≈ 0.6321.
  3. The mean waiting time is 1/0.5 = 2 minutes.
