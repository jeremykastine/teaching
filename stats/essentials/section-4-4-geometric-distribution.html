4.4: Geometric Distribution · Essentials

Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What does X count in this course's geometric model?
  2. What assumptions are required?
  3. What is P(X = k)?
  4. What is P(X > k)?
  5. What are mean and SD?
Check short-answer key
  1. The trial number of the first success, beginning at 1.
  2. Independent trials with a constant success probability p, where 0 < p ≤ 1.
  3. (1−p)k−1p, for k = 1, 2, … .
  4. (1−p)k, the probability of k initial failures.
  5. 1/p and 1−p/p, respectively.

Example problems

Example 1

With p = 0.25, find the probabilities of first success on trials 1, 2, and 3.

Show solution
  1. Trial 1: 0.25.
  2. Trial 2: 0.75(0.25) = 0.1875.
  3. Trial 3: (0.75)²(0.25) = 0.140625; the pattern is p, qp, q²p.

Example 2

With p = 0.2, find P(X > 3) and E(X).

Show solution
  1. X > 3 means the first three trials all fail.
  2. Probability = (0.8)³ = 0.512.
  3. Mean trial number = 1/0.2 = 5.
