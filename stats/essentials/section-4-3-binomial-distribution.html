Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What does a binomial random variable count?
  2. What parameters define it?
  3. What are its possible values?
  4. What is P(X = k)?
  5. What are its mean and standard deviation?
Check short-answer key
  1. Successes in a fixed number of independent two-outcome trials with constant success probability.
  2. Number of trials n and success probability p.
  3. 0, 1, …, n.
  4. (nk)pk(1−p)n−k.
  5. μ=np and σ=np(1−p).

Example problems

Example 1

For three independent trials with p = 0.5, find P(exactly two successes).

Show solution
  1. There are (32)=3 success/failure arrangements.
  2. Each has probability (0.5)³ = 1/8.
  3. Total probability = 3/8 = 0.375.

Example 2

For a binomial model with n = 20 and p = 0.3, find mean and SD.

Show solution
  1. Mean = np = 20(0.3) = 6 successes.
  2. Variance = 20(0.3)(0.7) = 4.2.
  3. SD = √4.2 ≈ 2.05 successes.
