Statistics

2.6: Skewness and the Mean, Median, and Mode · Essentials

Answer the questions from memory on paper. Then check the answer key. Attempt each example before opening its solution.

Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. How is skewness named?
  2. Which way does a right-skewed distribution's tail extend?
  3. How do outliers affect the mean compared with the median?
  4. What relationship often holds between mean and median under right skew?
  5. Does symmetry guarantee a single mode?
Check short-answer key
  1. By the direction of the longer tail.
  2. Toward larger values.
  3. The mean is generally pulled more strongly toward extreme values.
  4. Mean is often greater than median; this is a heuristic, not an infallible rule.
  5. No; symmetric distributions can be multimodal.

Example problems

Example 1

Find mean and median for 1, 2, 2, 3, 12, and describe the tail.

Show solution
  1. The sum is 20, so mean = 4.
  2. Median = 2.
  3. The large value 12 extends the right tail and pulls the mean above the median.

Example 2

Describe the shape of 0, 0, 2, 4, 4.

Show solution
  1. The values are symmetric about 2.
  2. Mean and median are both 2.
  3. Both 0 and 4 are modes, showing symmetry does not require one central mode.
