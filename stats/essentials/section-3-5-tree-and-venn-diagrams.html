Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What does a branch probability in a tree describe?
  2. How is a complete path probability found?
  3. How are probabilities of disjoint qualifying paths combined?
  4. What does the overlap in a Venn diagram represent?
  5. What belongs outside both circles but inside the sample space?
Check short-answer key
  1. The probability of the next outcome conditional on that branch's history.
  2. Multiply conditional probabilities along the path.
  3. Add them.
  4. Outcomes in both events.
  5. Outcomes in neither event.

Example problems

Example 1

A bag has 2 red and 1 blue object. Draw two without replacement. Find P(exactly one red) using paths.

Show solution
  1. Red then blue has probability (2/3)(1/2) = 1/3.
  2. Blue then red has probability (1/3)(2/2) = 1/3.
  3. Add these disjoint paths to get 2/3.

Example 2

Of 30 people, 18 like tea, 14 like coffee, and 8 like both. Fill the Venn regions.

Show solution
  1. Tea only: 18 − 8 = 10; coffee only: 14 − 8 = 6.
  2. Both: 8; total in either: 10 + 8 + 6 = 24.
  3. Neither: 30 − 24 = 6.
