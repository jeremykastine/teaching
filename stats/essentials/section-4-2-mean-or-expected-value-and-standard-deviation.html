4.2: Mean or Expected Value and Standard Deviation · Essentials

Answer the questions from memory on paper. Then check the answer key. Attempt each example before opening its solution.

Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What is the expected value of a discrete random variable?
  2. Must an expected value be a possible individual outcome?
  3. What is its population variance?
  4. What is its standard deviation?
  5. What does a larger standard deviation mean in a payoff comparison?
Check short-answer key
  1. μ=∑xxP(X=x).
  2. No; it is a long-run average under the model.
  3. σ2=∑x(x−μ)2P(X=x).
  4. The square root of its variance, in the same units as X.
  5. Greater variability in payoff; it does not by itself determine which option is preferred.

Example problems

Example 1

A net payoff is −$2 with probability 0.75 or $6 with probability 0.25. Find expected payoff and SD.

Show solution
  1. Mean: (−2)(0.75) + 6(0.25) = $0.
  2. Variance: (−2 − 0)²(0.75) + (6 − 0)²(0.25) = 12 dollars squared.
  3. SD = √12 ≈ $3.46.

Example 2

Compare a guaranteed $2 with a payoff of $0 or $4, each with probability 1/2.

Show solution
  1. Both means are $2.
  2. The guarantee has SD 0. The variable payoff has variance (0 − 2)²/2 + (4 − 2)²/2 = 4 and SD $2.
  3. A person preferring less variability at the same mean would choose the guarantee.
