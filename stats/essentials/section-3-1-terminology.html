Example problems

Example 1

Roll a fair six-sided die. Give the event of an even number and its probability.

Show solution
  1. Sample space: {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}; event: {2, 4, 6}.
  2. Three of six equally likely outcomes are favorable.
  3. Probability = 3/6 = 1/2.
