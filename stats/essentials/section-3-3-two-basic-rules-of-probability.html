Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. State the general addition rule.
  2. State the general multiplication rule.
  3. Why subtract the overlap in the addition rule?
  4. What is the complement rule?
  5. When may P(B|A) be replaced by P(B)?
Check short-answer key
  1. P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A and B).
  2. P(A and B) = P(A)P(B|A), when P(A) > 0.
  3. It was counted once in each individual probability.
  4. P(not A) = 1 − P(A).
  5. When A and B are independent.

Example problems

Example 1

If P(A) = 0.4, P(B) = 0.3, and P(A and B) = 0.1, find P(A or B).

Show solution
  1. Add individual probabilities and subtract the overlap.
  2. 0.4 + 0.3 − 0.1 = 0.6.

Example 2

A bag has 3 red and 2 blue objects. Draw two without replacement. Find P(both red).

Show solution
  1. The first-red probability is 3/5.
  2. After a red is removed, the second-red probability is 2/4.
  3. Multiply: (3/5)(2/4) = 3/10.
