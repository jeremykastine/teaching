Statistics

7.2: The Central Limit Theorem for Sums · Essentials

Answer the questions from memory on paper. Then check the answer key. Attempt each example before opening its solution.

Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What is the expected sum of n independent identically distributed observations with mean μ?
  2. What is the variance of their sum if each has variance σ²?
  3. What is the SD of their sum?
  4. How is a sum related to the sample mean?
  5. What is essential when adding variances without covariance terms?
Check short-answer key
  1. nμ.
  2. nσ².
  3. σn.
  4. S = n times the sample mean.
  5. Independence, or at least zero covariance with suitable finite variances.

Example problems

Example 1

Independent package weights have mean 2 kg and SD 0.3 kg. Find mean and SD for a total of 100 packages.

Show solution
  1. Mean total = 100(2) = 200 kg.
  2. Variance total = 100(0.3²) = 9 kg squared.
  3. SD total = 3 kg.

Example 2

Assume individual measurements are independent normal with mean 10 and SD 2. For 16 measurements, find P(sum > 176).

Show solution
  1. The sum is normal with mean 160 and SD 2√16 = 8.
  2. z = (176 − 160)/8 = 2.
  3. Probability ≈ 0.0228.
