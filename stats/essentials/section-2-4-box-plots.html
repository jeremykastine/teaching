Statistics

2.4: Box Plots · Essentials

Answer the questions from memory on paper. Then check the answer key. Attempt each example before opening its solution.

Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What five numbers summarize an ordinary box plot?
  2. What does the box span?
  3. What are the usual outlier fences?
  4. Where do whiskers end on a modified box plot?
  5. Does an outlier flag prove a recording error?
Check short-answer key
  1. Minimum, Q₁, median, Q₃, maximum.
  2. Q₁ to Q₃, the middle half of the distribution approximately.
  3. Q₁ − 1.5 IQR and Q₃ + 1.5 IQR.
  4. At the most extreme observed values within the fences.
  5. No; investigate it in context.

Example problems

Example 1

With Q₁ = 10 and Q₃ = 18, find fences and classify 31.

Show solution
  1. IQR = 18 − 10 = 8.
  2. Fences: 10 − 12 = −2 and 18 + 12 = 30.
  3. 31 exceeds the upper fence, so it is flagged as an outlier.

Example 2

Describe an ordinary box plot with five-number summary 2, 4, 7, 10, 15.

Show solution
  1. Draw a box from 4 to 10 with a median line at 7.
  2. Draw whiskers to 2 and 15.
  3. The IQR is 10 − 4 = 6 and the range is 15 − 2 = 13.
