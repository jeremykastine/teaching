U1-G01: Sampling scope and variables

U1-001. A library surveys only people entering the library to estimate how often all students visit. What is the main concern?

  1. The sample must be too small.
  2. Randomly ordering the responses removes the concern.
  3. Students who never visit are excluded.
  4. Every recorded visit count is wrong.

Slides 1.1, page 21: A question of scope · evaluate

Reveal answer and explanation

C. Students who never visit are excluded.

Selection excludes a relevant part of the population, regardless of sample size.

U1-002. A random sample of 80 enrolled students reports 30% support for later hours. Which conclusion is justified?

  1. Exactly 24 students in the entire college support later hours.
  2. Exactly 30% of all students support later hours.
  3. 30% describes the sample and estimates population support.
  4. The population percentage must exceed 30%.

Slides 1.1, page 3: Inferential statistics · interpret

Reveal answer and explanation

C. 30% describes the sample and estimates population support.

A sample result is evidence about a population, not an exact census result.

Reveal answer and explanation
