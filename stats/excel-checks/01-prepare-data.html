Match Mean, Range, and Spread

Loading your problem…

Make an Excel spreadsheet that helps you guess and check which observations would have these outcomes.

Requirements

  • A new Excel workbook with exactly ten of your own values in A2:A11, one observation per cell, all between 0 and 1,000 inclusive. Decimals are allowed.
  • An exact match for the count and range.
  • A mean and the requested standard deviation or variance approximately equal to their targets, close enough to satisfy your instructor. The spread target is rounded to four decimal places.
  • Labeled, live formulas from left to right: observations, a column repeating the current mean, deviations from that mean, squared deviations, their sum, the correct division, and a square root only if standard deviation is requested.
  • Calculate the mean with AVERAGE of the entire observation column and fill it down. Use SUM and division by n for a population or n − 1 for a sample. Show the spread calculation step by step rather than using a built-in variance or standard-deviation shortcut.
  • A separate range calculation using MAX minus MIN, and a COUNT formula to confirm ten observations. All calculations must update when your observations change.
