These original discussion prompts follow the objectives of OpenStax *College Algebra 2e*, Jay Abramson (2021). Each slide is a definition, a concept, or an unsolved problem with a focused question. Chapter 2 has 143 slides; Chapter 3 has 157. The approved portrait format and clear lower annotation area are retained.

| 3.1 | 24 | Functions from relations; notation; evaluate outputs and solve for inputs; one-to-one behavior; vertical and horizontal line tests; toolkit graphs. | Distinguish evaluation from solving; substitute a complete expression into a function; form a simple difference quotient. ## Content and rendering checks

Every slide has the existing `title`, `body`, `math`, and `question` fields; every question ends with a question mark. No worked solutions or narration are included. Body text is at most 45 words and questions at most 25 words.

All 300 formulas/prompts passed independent checks using the same 28-point Matplotlib mathtext formula rendering and 22-point ReportLab paragraph wrapping as the approved builder. The largest formula is 378 points wide within the 516-point content area. The lowest prompt ends at 441.82 points from the bottom, above the 396-point midpoint. These checks supplement the root workflow's final generated-PDF and visual checks.

Piecewise rules use brief conventional notation in body text; no unsupported `cases`, `aligned`, or array renderer is required. The formulas use supported mathtext commands. Problems that ask students to sketch graphs leave the plotting and explanation to class discussion and pen annotations.
