Example problems

Example 1

Invert f(x) = (x − 2)² + 1 on x ≥ 2.

Show solution
  1. Swap x and y: x = (y − 2)² + 1.
  2. Since y must be at least 2, take y − 2 = √(x − 1).
  3. The inverse is 2 + √(x − 1), with domain [1, ∞).

Example 2

Invert f(x) = x³ − 4.

Show solution
  1. Swap: x = y³ − 4.
  2. Add 4 and take the cube root: f−1(x)=x+43.
  3. Both domain and range are all real numbers.
