Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What is a power function?
  2. Which exponents are allowed in a polynomial?
  3. How is polynomial degree determined?
  4. What determines polynomial end behavior?
  5. How many turning points can a degree-n polynomial have at most?
Check short-answer key
  1. A function of the form kxᵖ with constant k and p on its allowed real domain.
  2. Nonnegative integers.
  3. By the highest power with a nonzero coefficient after simplification.
  4. The degree's parity and the sign of the leading coefficient.
  5. n − 1.

Example problems

Example 1

Describe the end behavior of y=−2x4+3x−1.

Show solution
  1. The leading term is −2x⁴: even degree and negative coefficient.
  2. As x tends to either positive or negative infinity, y tends to negative infinity.
  3. Both ends point down.

Example 2

Identify which of 3x5, 2x, and x2+1/x are polynomial functions.

Show solution
  1. 3x⁵ is a polynomial of degree 5 and also a power function.
  2. 2ˣ has the variable in the exponent, so is exponential.
  3. x² + 1/x includes x⁻¹, so is not a polynomial.
