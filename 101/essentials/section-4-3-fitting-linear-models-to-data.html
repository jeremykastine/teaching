Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What does a scatter plot display?
  2. What is a residual?
  3. What does a least-squares line minimize?
  4. What is interpolation?
  5. Does strong correlation establish causation?
Check short-answer key
  1. Paired numerical observations as points.
  2. Observed y minus predicted y.
  3. The sum of squared vertical residuals.
  4. Prediction within the observed input range.
  5. No; association alone does not show cause.

Example problems

Example 1

A fitted model is y = 2x + 5. An observation is (4, 15). Find its prediction and residual.

Show solution
  1. Predicted y = 2(4) + 5 = 13.
  2. Residual = 15 − 13 = 2.
  3. The observation is 2 output units above the fitted line.

Example 2

Data were collected for x from 10 to 20. Classify predictions at 15 and 30.

Show solution
  1. At 15 the prediction is interpolation, within the observed range.
  2. At 30 it is extrapolation.
  3. Extrapolation needs extra justification because the pattern may change beyond the data.
