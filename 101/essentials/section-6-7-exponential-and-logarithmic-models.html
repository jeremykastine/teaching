Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What is a continuous exponential growth or decay model?
  2. What does the sign of k indicate?
  3. What is doubling time for k > 0?
  4. What is half-life for k < 0?
  5. What does the carrying capacity of a logistic model describe?
Check short-answer key
  1. A(t) = A₀eᵏᵗ.
  2. Positive means growth; negative means decay.
  3. ln 2 divided by k.
  4. ln 2 divided by |k|.
  5. Its limiting population level under the model.

Example problems

Example 1

A quantity starts at 80 and doubles every 3 hours. Find its value after 9 hours.

Show solution
  1. Write A(t)=80·2t/3.
  2. Nine hours is three doubling periods.
  3. A(9) = 80 × 8 = 640.

Example 2

A sample starts at 100 g with a 4-year half-life. Find the mass after 12 years.

Show solution
  1. Write A(t)=100(1/2)t/4.
  2. Twelve years is three half-lives.
  3. Remaining mass = 100/8 = 12.5 g.
