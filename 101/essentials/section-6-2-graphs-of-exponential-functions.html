6.2: Graphs of Exponential Functions · Essentials

Answer the questions from memory on paper. Then check the answer key. Attempt each example before opening its solution.

Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What is the domain of bˣ for b > 0?
  2. What is its range when b ≠ 1?
  3. What is the horizontal asymptote of abˣ + k for a ≠ 0?
  4. How does a negative a affect abˣ?
  5. What happens to the y-intercept after a vertical shift?
Check short-answer key
  1. All real numbers.
  2. (0, ∞).
  3. y = k.
  4. It reflects the graph across the x-axis relative to positive |a|.
  5. The shift is added to the original y-intercept.

Example problems

Example 1

Analyze y = 2ˣ + 3.

Show solution
  1. Shift the basic growth curve up 3.
  2. Domain: all reals; range: (3, ∞); horizontal asymptote: y = 3.
  3. At x = 0, y = 4, so the y-intercept is (0, 4).

Example 2

Analyze y = −(1/2)ˣ + 1.

Show solution
  1. Reflect the decay curve across the x-axis, then shift up 1.
  2. Domain: all reals; range: (−∞, 1); horizontal asymptote: y = 1.
  3. The graph increases and passes through (0, 0).
