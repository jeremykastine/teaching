Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What is the form of a linear function?
  2. What does its slope measure?
  3. How do you find slope from two points?
  4. What slope relationship holds for parallel nonvertical lines?
  5. What relationship holds for perpendicular nonvertical lines with nonzero slopes?
Check short-answer key
  1. f(x) = mx + b.
  2. Constant change in output per unit change in input.
  3. Change in y divided by change in x.
  4. Equal slopes.
  5. Their slopes multiply to −1.

Example problems

Example 1

Find the line through (−1, 4) and (3, −4).

Show solution
  1. Slope: (−4 − 4)/(3 − (−1)) = −2.
  2. Substitute (−1, 4): 4 = −2(−1) + b, giving b = 2.
  3. The equation is y = −2x + 2.

Example 2

Find a line perpendicular to y = 2x + 1 through (0, 3).

Show solution
  1. The perpendicular slope is −1/2.
  2. The point gives y-intercept 3.
  3. The line is y = −x/2 + 3.
