Example problems

Example 1

A taxi charges $4 plus $2 per mile. How many miles cost $18?

Show solution
  1. Let m be miles: 4 + 2m = 18.
  2. Solve 2m = 14, so m = 7 miles.
  3. Check: $4 + $14 = $18.

Example 2

How long does a 150-mile trip take at a constant 60 mph?

Show solution
  1. Use 150 = 60t with t in hours.
  2. Divide: t = 2.5 hours.
  3. This assumes constant speed and excludes stops.
