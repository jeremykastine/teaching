3.5: Transformation of Functions · Essentials

Answer the questions from memory on paper. Then check the answer key. Attempt each example before opening its solution.

Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What does f(x) + k do to a graph?
  2. What does f(x − h) do?
  3. What does −f(x) do?
  4. What does f(−x) do?
  5. What does af(x), with a > 1, do?
Check short-answer key
  1. Shift it vertically by k.
  2. Shift it horizontally right by h when h > 0.
  3. Reflect across the x-axis.
  4. Reflect across the y-axis.
  5. Stretch all vertical distances from the x-axis by factor a.

Example problems

Example 1

Describe y = −2(x − 3)² + 1 from y = x².

Show solution
  1. Shift right 3, stretch vertically by 2, reflect across the x-axis, then shift up 1.
  2. The vertex is (3, 1), and the parabola opens downward.

Example 2

A point (2, 5) is on y = f(x). Where does it move on y = f(x + 1) − 4?

Show solution
  1. x + 1 shifts the graph left 1.
  2. Subtracting 4 shifts it down 4.
  3. The point becomes (1, 1).
