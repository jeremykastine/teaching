Check short-answer key
  1. aman=am+n.
  2. am/an=am−n.
  3. A reciprocal: a−n=1/an, for a ≠ 0.
  4. (am)n=amn.
  5. a×10n, with 1≤|a|<10 and integer n.

Example problems

Example 1

Simplify (2x3)2/x4, x ≠ 0.

Show solution
  1. Square the numerator to get 4x6.
  2. Subtract exponents in the quotient: 4x6−4=4x2.

Example 2

Multiply (3×105)(2×10−3).

Show solution
  1. Multiply coefficients: 3 × 2 = 6.
  2. Add exponents: 5 + (−3) = 2.
  3. Result: 6×102=600.
