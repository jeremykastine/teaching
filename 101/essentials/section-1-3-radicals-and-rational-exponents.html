Example problems

Example 1

Simplify 50+28.

Show solution
  1. 50=52 and 8=22.
  2. Combine: 52+42=92.

Example 2

Evaluate 272/3 and rationalize 3/5.

Show solution
  1. Take the cube root of 27 and square: 272/3=32=9.
  2. Multiply the second expression by 5/5.
  3. Result: 35/5.
