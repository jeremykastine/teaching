1.4: Polynomials · Essentials

Answer the questions from memory on paper. Then check the answer key. Attempt each example before opening its solution.

Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What is a polynomial in x?
  2. What is a polynomial's degree?
  3. What are like terms?
  4. How do you subtract a polynomial?
  5. How do you multiply polynomials?
Check short-answer key
  1. A finite sum of terms with real coefficients and nonnegative integer exponents of x.
  2. Its largest exponent with a nonzero coefficient, for a nonzero polynomial.
  3. Terms with identical variable factors and exponents.
  4. Distribute the subtraction to every term, then combine like terms.
  5. Distribute every term of one across every term of the other.

Example problems

Example 1

Subtract (2x2−3x+1)−(x2+4x−5).

Show solution
  1. Distribute the minus: 2x2−3x+1−x2−4x+5.
  2. Combine: x2−7x+6.

Example 2

Expand (x−4)(2x+3).

Show solution
  1. Multiply pairs to obtain 2x2+3x−8x−12.
  2. Combine the middle terms: 2x2−5x−12.
