Essential facts, definitions, and procedures

  1. What distinguishes rational from irrational real numbers?
  2. State the distributive property.
  3. What is the additive inverse of a?
  4. What is absolute value?
  5. What is the order of operations?
Check short-answer key
  1. Rational numbers are ratios of integers with nonzero denominator; irrational numbers are not.
  2. a(b+c)=ab+ac.
  3. −a, because a + (−a) = 0.
  4. Distance from zero on the number line.
  5. Grouping, exponents, multiplication/division left to right, then addition/subtraction left to right.

Example problems

Example 1

Evaluate 3−2(5−8)2.

Show solution
  1. Parentheses give −3, whose square is 9.
  2. Multiply: 2 × 9 = 18.
  3. Subtract: 3 − 18 = −15.

Example 2

Classify −7, 3/8, and 2 as rational or irrational.

Show solution
  1. −7 = −7/1 and 3/8 are ratios of integers, so both are rational.
  2. √2 cannot be expressed as such a ratio, so it is irrational.
  3. All three are real numbers.
